How Your HELOC Rate Is Set

Your rate = prime rate + the lender's margin. Prime moves with the Fed. The margin is fixed for the life of the line and is the only part you can shop for. With prime at 7.00%, the national average of 7.83% implies a typical margin of about 0.83 of a point.

What moves your margin

  • Credit score: the best margins usually need a score in the mid-700s or higher; many lenders will not lend below about 620 to 680.
  • Combined loan-to-value (CLTV): your mortgage plus the credit line, divided by the home's value. Most lenders stop at 80% to 85%, and price best below about 70%.
  • Debt-to-income ratio: commonly capped around 43%.
  • Line size and property type: larger lines and owner-occupied homes price better than small lines, second homes or rentals.
  • Relationship and autopay discounts: often a quarter-point each.
  • Lender type: credit unions frequently post lower margins than large banks.

How Much Can You Borrow?

Maximum line = (home value × the lender's CLTV limit) − mortgage balance.

Example: a home worth $400,000 with $220,000 owed. At an 80% limit the maximum line is $100,000; at 85%, $120,000.

The Costs Beyond the Rate

  • Introductory rates: a low rate for 6 to 12 months, then prime plus margin. Compare the margin, not the teaser.
  • Rate floor: a minimum rate your line can never go below, however far prime falls.
  • Lifetime cap: the maximum rate, which the lender must disclose. Often 18%.
  • Fees: appraisal or valuation, origination, an annual fee (often $50 to $100), and an early-closure fee if you close the line in the first two or three years.
  • Minimum draw: some lenders require an initial draw or a minimum balance.
  • Fixed-rate lock: many lenders let you convert part of the balance to a fixed rate. Useful when rates are rising, as they are now.

Is HELOC Interest Tax-Deductible?

Only if the money is used to buy, build or substantially improve the home that secures the line, you itemise deductions, and your total mortgage debt, including the HELOC, is within $750,000 ($375,000 if married filing separately). Interest on HELOC money used for a car, tuition, debt consolidation or a holiday is not deductible.

Risks to Weigh

  • Your home is the collateral. Missed payments can end in foreclosure.
  • The rate is variable. It follows the Fed up as well as down.
  • Payment shock at the end of the draw period (table above).
  • The lender can freeze or cut the line if your home's value falls significantly or your finances change materially.
  • It reduces your equity, which matters if you need to sell in a soft market.

You have a legal right to cancel a HELOC on your main home within three business days of opening it, at no cost.

How to Get the Lowest HELOC Rate

  1. Check your credit reports and correct errors before applying.
  2. Get quotes from at least three lenders on the same day, including a credit union and your current mortgage lender. Ask each for the margin, the floor, the lifetime cap and every fee.
  3. Ask what CLTV gets the best pricing, and whether a slightly smaller line moves you into it.
  4. Ask for relationship and autopay discounts.
  5. Ask whether part of the balance can be fixed later, and at what rate.
  6. Read the early-closure and annual fees before you sign, and use the three-day cancellation right if something is different at closing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good HELOC rate right now?

With prime at 7.00%, anything at or below about 7.50% is good, and the average is about 7.83%. Below prime is exceptional and usually an introductory offer.

What is the monthly payment on a $100,000 HELOC?

At 7.83%, $652.50 a month interest-only during the draw period, and $825.89 a month when repaying over 20 years.

How much is the monthly payment on a $50,000 HELOC?

At 7.83%, $326.25 interest-only, or $412.95 over a 20-year repayment period.

Is a HELOC a bad idea right now?

Not for planned spending that improves the home and that you can repay. Be cautious about variable-rate borrowing for everyday expenses when the Fed has just raised rates.

Is there a better alternative to a HELOC?

A fixed-rate home equity loan currently averages 7.50%, below the average HELOC, and its payment cannot rise. For a one-off cost it is often the better choice.

Does it make sense to pay off a mortgage with a HELOC?

Rarely. You would swap a fixed rate for a variable one. It can work only for a small remaining balance you will clear quickly at a lower rate.

Will HELOC rates go down in 2027?

They will follow the Federal Reserve. Nobody can know its path; build your budget on today's rate plus at least one percentage point.

How fast do HELOC rates change after a Fed decision?

Prime usually changes the next business day, and your HELOC rate follows at its next adjustment, normally within one or two billing cycles.

Do I need an appraisal for a HELOC?

Usually some form of valuation: a full appraisal, a drive-by, or an automated estimate for smaller lines.
