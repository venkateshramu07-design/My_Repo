Updated 10 November 2026

Quick answer: The average auto loan rate is 6.35% for a new car and 11.19% for a used car, according to Experian's figures for the second quarter of 2026, the latest full quarter published. Your credit score moves the rate far more than the lender does: borrowers with scores of 781 or higher average 4.41% on a new car, and those at 500 or below average 16.11%. On a $40,000 six-year loan that difference is $17,049 in interest. Rates are unlikely to fall soon: the Federal Reserve raised its benchmark rate on 16 September 2026.

One percentage point on this loan is worth $1,375 over six years. That is the number to keep in mind when a dealer says a rate difference is "only a few dollars a month".

The dealer markup

When a dealer arranges your loan, the lender quotes the dealer a "buy rate" and the dealer is usually allowed to add to it and keep the difference, often up to about two percentage points. On the example loan, a two-point markup costs $2,773. You remove it by arriving with a preapproval and asking the dealer to beat it.

How to Get a Lower Rate: A Checklist

  1. Check your credit a few weeks before you shop and correct errors. Paying card balances down before the statement date can lift a score quickly.
  2. Get preapproved by two or three lenders, including a credit union, within the same two weeks. Credit scoring models treat auto loan enquiries made close together (14 to 45 days, depending on the model) as one.
  3. Negotiate the price of the car first, then the financing, then the trade-in. Do not negotiate on monthly payment.
  4. Ask the dealer to beat your preapproval. Ask what the buy rate is.
  5. Compare a promotional rate against the cash rebate. Take whichever leaves the lower total cost, using your own preapproval rate for the rebate option.
  6. Choose the shortest term whose payment you can afford and put down enough to avoid owing more than the car is worth.
  7. Decline add-ons rolled into the loan unless you would pay cash for them.
  8. Refinance later if your score has risen a tier. Do not extend the term when you do.

The New Car Loan Interest Deduction

For tax years 2025 to 2028, interest on a loan for a qualifying new vehicle is deductible, up to $10,000 a year, whether or not you itemise.

  • Vehicle: new, for personal use, with final assembly in the United States. Used cars and leases do not qualify.
  • Loan: taken out after 31 December 2024 and secured by the vehicle.
  • Income limit: the deduction shrinks by $200 for every $1,000 of modified adjusted gross income above $100,000 ($200,000 for joint filers).
  • Paperwork: claimed on Schedule 1-A with the vehicle identification number. Lenders report interest of $600 or more on Form 1098-VLI.

On the $40,000, 6.15% example, first-year interest is about $2,301. In the 22% tax bracket the deduction is worth about $506 that year, the equivalent of cutting the rate to roughly 4.8%. It lowers the cost of a loan you were going to take; it is not a reason to borrow more.

Why Rates Are Where They Are

Auto loan rates follow the Federal Reserve's benchmark with a lag. The Fed raised its target range to 3.75% to 4.00% on 16 September 2026 and held it on 28 October; its next decision is on 9 December. Experian's Q2 figures were recorded before that increase, so current offers may run slightly above the averages in this article. Experian reported an average new-car payment of $765 a month in the same quarter.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good auto loan rate right now?

At or below the average for your credit tier: about 4.41% (new) with a score above 780, and about 6.15% with a score from 661 to 780. For used cars, about 6.29% and 8.81%.

What is the average auto loan rate for good credit?

For prime borrowers (661 to 780), 6.15% on new cars and 8.81% on used cars.

Is there a 0% interest car loan?

Yes, from manufacturers' finance arms on selected new models, for borrowers with top-tier credit, usually over shorter terms and instead of a cash rebate.

Can I get a 1.9% interest rate on a car loan?

Only through a manufacturer promotion with excellent credit. Banks and credit unions are not lending at that rate at today's benchmark rates.

What is a good APR for a 72-month car loan?

Around the average for your tier, or a little above it, because long terms are priced higher. For prime credit that means roughly 6% to 7% on a new car.

Can I negotiate my car loan rate?

Yes. Dealer-arranged rates often include a markup. A preapproval from another lender is the strongest way to remove it.

Which bank has the lowest auto loan rates?

It changes week to week and depends on your credit. Credit unions are the most consistent place to find the lowest rate; collect at least three quotes on the same day.

Does applying for several car loans hurt my credit?

Applications made within about two weeks are counted as a single enquiry by the main scoring models.

Should I finance through the dealer or my bank?

Get your own preapproval first, then let the dealer try to beat it. Take whichever is cheaper in total.
