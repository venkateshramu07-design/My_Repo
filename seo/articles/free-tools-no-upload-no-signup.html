How to Tell Whether an Online Tool Is Really Free and Private

  • Try the download first. Before typing anything real, make a test version and download it. If the download asks for payment or an account, leave.
  • Look for "no upload" or "processed in your browser". Better still, load the page, turn off your internet connection and see whether it still works.
  • Read what happens after a trial. Dynamic QR codes, e-signature sends and "pro" exports often stop or start charging when the trial ends.
  • Don't upload ID, contracts or medical records to any site that processes files on its own servers, whatever its privacy page says.
