Quick answer: To merge, split or reorder a PDF without uploading it anywhere, use a tool that runs inside your browser. The toolkit below opens your PDFs (and photos) on your own device, shows every page, and lets you drag pages into order, rotate or delete them, pick a range, and download one combined PDF, just the pages you ticked, or every page as a separate file. Nothing is uploaded, so it is safe for contracts, bank statements, medical forms and ID documents, and there is no sign-up, daily limit or watermark.

Most "free" online PDF sites work by uploading your file to their server, doing the job there and sending it back. That has two costs. The first is privacy: your tax return or passport scan now sits, at least for a while, on a company's computer, under its retention policy. One popular converter's own privacy page tells users not to upload contracts, financial statements or medical records. The second is the limits. Comparisons of the big names list caps such as two free tasks a day, three an hour or one an hour, plus file-size limits and watermarks, all designed to push you to a subscription.

What You Can Do With It

  • Merge PDFs: add two or more files and press "Download as one PDF". The pages come out in the order shown.
  • Reorder pages: drag a page to a new position, or use the arrow buttons on phones.
  • Rotate pages: fix sideways scans one page at a time, or tick several and press "Rotate ticked".
  • Delete pages: remove blank or unwanted pages before sending.
  • Extract pages: type a range such as 1-3, 7 and press Tick, then "Download ticked pages".
  • Split a PDF: "Split into one PDF per page" gives a zip file with each page as its own PDF.
  • Turn photos into a PDF: add JPG, PNG or WebP photos (for example pictures of receipts or a signed form) and they become A4 pages in the same document.

How to Split a PDF Free, Step by Step

  1. Tap Choose PDFs or photos and pick your file. It opens in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
  2. To pull out some pages, tick them (or type a page range), then tap Download ticked pages.
  3. To split every page into its own file, tap Split into one PDF per page (.zip).
  4. To merge instead, add several PDFs or photos, drag the pages into order and tap Download as one PDF.

How It Keeps Your Files Private

The page loads two well-known open-source libraries, pdf-lib to edit PDFs and Mozilla's pdf.js to draw the page previews. After that, all the work happens in your browser's memory. You can check this yourself: once the page has loaded, turn on airplane mode and the tool still works. When you close the tab, the files are gone; nothing is saved.

Tips for Common Jobs

  • Visa, loan or job applications often ask for everything in one PDF. Add your form, scans and photos, put them in the order listed on the checklist, and download one file.
  • Sending only part of a statement: extract just the pages that are needed so you share less personal data.
  • Scans that come out sideways: rotate the pages here rather than rescanning. The rotation is saved in the file, so it opens the right way on any device.
  • Very large files: phones can run short of memory with hundreds of pages. If that happens, do it in two batches, or use a computer.

What It Can't Do

Password-protected PDFs can't be edited until the password is removed. Open the file with its password in your PDF viewer and save or print a copy without one, then add that copy. The toolkit also doesn't edit the text inside pages or compress a PDF's images; to make a scanned PDF much smaller, photograph or scan the pages at a lower resolution and turn the photos into a PDF here. Frequently Asked Questions: Split PDF

How do I merge PDF files for free without uploading them?

Open the toolkit above, add your PDFs, arrange the pages and press "Download as one PDF". The files are combined in your browser and never uploaded, and there is no limit or watermark.

Is it safe to merge confidential PDFs online?

It is safe when the tool processes files on your device, as this one does. Upload-based sites send your file to their servers, so avoid them for contracts, bank statements, tax and medical documents or ID.

How do I split a PDF into separate pages?

Add the PDF and press "Split into one PDF per page". You get a zip file containing every page as its own PDF. To keep only some pages, type a range like 2-5 and download the ticked pages instead.

Can I combine photos and PDFs into one file?

Yes. Add JPG, PNG or WebP photos together with PDFs. Each photo becomes an A4 page, and you can drag it anywhere in the document.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes, in Safari and Chrome. On phones, use the arrow buttons to move pages, since dragging is easier with a mouse.
