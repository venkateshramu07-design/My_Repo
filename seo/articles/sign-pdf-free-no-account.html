How to Sign a PDF in Four Steps

  1. Open the PDF. Choose the file or drag it onto the box. Every page appears below.
  2. Make your signature. Draw it in the box, or switch to Type and pick a handwriting style, or upload a photo of your signature on white paper (the background is removed automatically). Choose black or blue ink.
  3. Place it. Scroll to the page with the signature line and press "+ Signature". Drag it onto the line and pull the blue corner to resize it. Add "+ Date", "+ Initials" or "+ Text" for your printed name, just as you would on paper.
  4. Download. Press "Download signed PDF". Your original file is unchanged; the signed copy is saved as filename-signed.pdf, ready to email back.

Tick "Remember on this device" to keep your drawn signature in this browser for next time. It is stored only on your device, and unticking the box deletes it.

Is an Electronic Signature Legally Binding?

For most everyday documents, yes, as long as you intend to sign and the other party accepts electronic signatures:

  • United States: the federal ESIGN Act (and the UETA laws adopted by almost every state) says a signature or contract can't be refused legal effect just because it is electronic.
  • United Kingdom: the Law Commission's 2019 review confirmed that electronic signatures can be used to sign documents validly, including many that must be executed as deeds, subject to the usual formalities such as witnesses where those apply.
  • European Union: under the eIDAS regulation, a simple electronic signature can't be denied legal effect in court just because it is electronic. Some documents call for "advanced" or "qualified" signatures backed by identity checks, which this tool does not provide.
  • India and many other countries recognise electronic signatures in law too, but often require certified digital signatures for government filings and certain contracts.

The usual exceptions are wills, some property transfers and certain court and notarised documents. If a document says "wet ink signature required", print it and sign by hand. When in doubt, ask the person who sent it whether a signed PDF is acceptable; for leases, offers, consent forms, timesheets and invoices it almost always is.

Tips for a Clean Signature

  • On a phone, turn it sideways for a wider box, and sign slowly. The pad smooths the line as you go.
  • For an uploaded signature, use dark ink on plain white paper, in good light, without shadows.
  • Size the signature to sit on the line, roughly the height of two lines of text.
  • Check every page that needs initials. Many contracts ask for initials on each page and a full signature at the end.
