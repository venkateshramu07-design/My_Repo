How to Resize an Image to 50KB in 4 Steps

  1. Choose your photo in the tool above (or several at once). iPhone HEIC photos and PNG screenshots are fine; the result is always a real JPG.
  2. Check the limit: the tool is set to 50 KB, with a minimum of 20 KB. If the form also gives a minimum, such as “20 to 50 KB”, type it in the second box so the file is not too small either.
  3. Set the dimensions only if the form gives them. Leave “Keep shape” selected otherwise: the tool shrinks the photo just enough to stay sharp. For an SSC photo, choose cm and 3.5 × 4.5; for IBPS, choose pixels and 200 × 230.
  4. Download the JPG (or all of them as a .zip) and upload it. The result line shows the exact KB, the size in pixels and the JPEG quality it used.

How the Tool Picks the Size

A JPEG's file size depends on two things: how many pixels it has and how strongly it is compressed (the “quality” setting). Most compressors only lower the quality, which is why their 20 KB results look blocky. This tool searches for the highest quality that fits and, if that would fall below about 60%, makes the photo slightly smaller in pixels instead, because a smaller sharp photo looks far better on a form than a large blurry one. It also writes the resolution (dpi) into the file, which some government portals check, and counts 1 KB as 1,000 bytes so the file passes whichever way the portal measures.

Frequently Asked Questions: Resize Image to 50KB

How do I reduce a photo to 50 KB?

Choose it in the tool above and download; the limit is already 50 KB with a 20 KB minimum. Set the pixels or centimetres first if your form gives them.

How do I make a photo between 20 KB and 50 KB?

Keep the tool's settings (maximum 50, minimum 20). It aims close to 50 KB for sharpness and pads the file if it would come out under 20 KB.

What is a 50 KB photo size in pixels?

At good quality, about 750 × 615 pixels for a detailed camera photo and up to 1,300 pixels wide for a simpler one. Exam photos are much smaller (200 × 230 or 276 × 354 pixels), so they fit at high quality.

How do I compress an image to 50 KB on a phone?

Open this page on your phone, tap Choose photos, pick the photo from your gallery or take one, and download the result. It works on Android and iPhone, including HEIC photos.

Why does the SSC site reject my 50 KB photo?

Check the minimum (20 KB) and the dimensions, and make sure the file is a real JPG. Also note that many SSC forms now capture a live photo, so read the current notice.
