The online PAN application rejects photos that are too big, the wrong shape or saved at the wrong resolution. A phone photo is 3–5 MB; the PAN photo has to be under 20 KB. The tool below crops, resizes and compresses it to that size on your own device, and writes 200 dpi into the file.

How to Resize Your Photo for PAN in 4 Steps

  1. Take a recent colour photo against a plain white or light background, face straight to the camera, both ears visible, no cap or dark glasses.
  2. Crop it to head and shoulders in your phone's editor, so your face fills most of the picture. The tool keeps the 2.5 × 3.5 cm shape and fills the frame from the centre.
  3. Add it to the tool above and download the JPG. It will be 197 × 276 pixels, 200 dpi and under 20 KB.
  4. For the signature, sign on white paper, photograph it, set the limit to 10 KB, choose cm and enter 4.5 × 2, tick Black and white and download. Our [[post:articles/signature-resize|signature resizer]] does the same with more options.

Why the PAN Portal Rejects Photos

  • Over 20 KB: the most common error. The tool always stays under 20,000 bytes.
  • Wrong resolution: the portal reads the dpi stored inside the JPEG. The tool writes 200 dpi.
  • Not a real JPG: a renamed PNG or iPhone HEIC file fails. The tool always saves a genuine JPG and opens HEIC photos.
  • Signature in colour or on grey paper: tick Black and white so the background becomes pure white.

Frequently Asked Questions: Resize Photo for PAN Card

What is the photo size for a PAN card?

On Protean's online form: 2.5 × 3.5 cm at 200 dpi, colour, JPEG of up to 20 KB. At 200 dpi that is 197 × 276 pixels.

What is the signature size for a PAN card?

4.5 cm wide by 2 cm high at 200 dpi, black and white, JPEG of up to 10 KB (about 354 × 157 pixels).

How do I reduce my photo to 20 KB for PAN?

Use the tool above; it is preset to the PAN photo size and 20 KB. Add the photo and download.

What is 2.5 × 3.5 cm in pixels?

197 × 276 pixels at 200 dpi, 98 × 138 at 100 dpi and 295 × 413 at 300 dpi.

Can I use the same photo for the UTIITSL PAN form?

Usually, but check the limits shown on UTIITSL's upload screen and type them into the tool if they differ.
