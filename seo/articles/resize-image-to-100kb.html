A 100 KB limit is generous: a photo that size can still be 1,000 pixels or more across. The trouble is that phones save photos at 2–5 MB, so it still needs to shrink 20 to 50 times. The tool does that on your device, keeping as much detail as fits, and never uploads the photo.

How to Resize an Image to 100KB in 4 Steps

  1. Choose your photo in the tool above (or several at once). iPhone HEIC photos and PNG screenshots are fine; the result is always a real JPG.
  2. Check the limit: the tool is set to 100 KB. If the form also gives a minimum, such as “50 to 100 KB”, type it in the second box so the file is not too small either.
  3. Set the dimensions only if the form gives them. Leave “Keep shape” selected otherwise: the tool shrinks the photo just enough to stay sharp.
  4. Download the JPG (or all of them as a .zip) and upload it. The result line shows the exact KB, the size in pixels and the JPEG quality it used.

JPG, PNG or HEIC to 100 KB

The tool accepts JPG, PNG, WebP and iPhone HEIC files and always saves a JPG, because almost every form asks for JPG or JPEG (they are the same format). A PNG screenshot of a document can be several megabytes; as a JPG under 100 KB it stays perfectly readable. How the Tool Picks the Size

A JPEG's file size depends on two things: how many pixels it has and how strongly it is compressed (the “quality” setting). Most compressors only lower the quality, which is why their 20 KB results look blocky. This tool searches for the highest quality that fits and, if that would fall below about 60%, makes the photo slightly smaller in pixels instead, because a smaller sharp photo looks far better on a form than a large blurry one. It also writes the resolution (dpi) into the file, which some government portals check, and counts 1 KB as 1,000 bytes so the file passes whichever way the portal measures.

Frequently Asked Questions: Resize Image to 100KB

How do I compress a JPG to 100 KB?

Choose it in the tool above and download. The limit is set to 100 KB; the result line shows the final size, pixels and quality.

What is the size of a 100 KB photo in pixels?

At good quality, roughly 1,000 × 870 pixels for a detailed camera photo and up to 1,800 pixels wide for a simpler image.

How do I upload a photo for GST registration?

The GST portal asks for a JPEG of up to 100 KB for each photograph. Compress it with the tool above, then upload it in the Promoters or Authorised Signatory tab.

How do I reduce a photo to 100 KB for EPFO?

Use the tool with the limit at 100 KB and upload the JPG as the nominee photo in the e-nomination step on the EPFO member portal.

Is 100 KB a good size for a photo?

For online forms, yes. It keeps a photo sharp on screen and loads fast. For printing, use 500 KB or more.
