Quick answer on UK passport photo size: A printed UK passport photo must be 45 mm high by 35 mm wide, with your head 29–34 mm from chin to crown, in colour, against a plain cream or light grey background, and taken within the last month. For an online application you can use a digital photo of at least 600 × 750 pixels and between 50 KB and 10 MB, and you're allowed to take it yourself. Make either version free below, without uploading your photo anywhere.

HM Passport Office is strict about shadows and background colour: a white wall with a shadow behind your head is one of the most common reasons photos are sent back. Taking the photo facing a window and standing away from the wall fixes most problems.

Online Application: Taking Your Own Digital Photo

  1. Ask someone to take your photo in daylight against a plain light wall, from arm's length or more, at your eye level.
  2. Add it to the tool, keep "United Kingdom: passport" selected and choose "A digital photo to upload".
  3. Fit your face to the oval and check that the background check is green.
  4. Download the file and upload it in the GOV.UK passport application. The service checks the photo and tells you if it may be rejected.

If you use a photo booth or shop instead, many can give you a digital photo code to type into the online form.

Printed Photos for a Paper Form

Choose "Printed photos" to get a 4 × 6 inch (10 × 15 cm) sheet with eight 35 × 45 mm photos, and print it at actual size on photo paper. Keep the two photos you send loose, not stapled or glued to the form. If your application needs a countersignatory, they sign the back of one photo.

Frequently Asked Questions: UK Passport Photo Size

What are the UK passport photo dimensions?

A printed UK passport photo is 35 mm wide and 45 mm high, with the head 29–34 mm tall. For online applications the digital photo must be at least 600 pixels wide and 750 pixels high.

What colour should you not wear in a passport photo?

Avoid clothes close to the background colour, such as white, cream or light grey for a UK photo, because you can blend into the background. Darker or stronger colours work best.

Can I wear makeup in a UK passport photo?

Yes, everyday makeup is fine as long as your face looks natural and recognisable, with no glare. Avoid heavy makeup that changes your features.

What size is a UK passport photo?

45 mm high by 35 mm wide, with the head between 29 and 34 mm from chin to crown.

Can I take my own UK passport photo?

Yes. For online applications GOV.UK accepts a digital photo taken at home, as long as it meets the rules. Ask someone else to take it rather than taking a selfie.

Can I smile in my UK passport photo?

No. You need a plain expression with your mouth closed and eyes open.

What colour background is allowed?

Plain cream or light grey for printed photos, and a plain light colour for digital photos, with no shadows behind you.
