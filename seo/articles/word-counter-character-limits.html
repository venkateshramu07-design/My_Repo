How Reading and Speaking Time Are Worked Out

Reading time uses 238 words a minute, the average silent reading speed for non-fiction found by a large 2019 review of 190 studies of adult readers in English. Speaking time uses 140 words a minute, a comfortable pace for a presentation or speech, so a 5-minute talk is about 700 words. Fast readers and fast talkers will finish sooner, and technical text takes longer.

The Clean-Up Tools

  • Sentence case fixes text typed with Caps Lock on, or copied in capitals, and capitalises the first letter of each sentence.
  • Title Case capitalises each main word for headlines, leaving short words like "of", "and" and "the" in lower case.
  • Remove extra spaces deletes double spaces, tabs and spaces at the ends of lines, which is common in text copied from PDFs.
  • Join lines into paragraphs repairs text from PDFs and emails where every line ends with a break, keeping real paragraph breaks.
  • Remove duplicate lines and Sort lines A–Z tidy lists of names, emails or keywords.
  • Straight quotes turns curly quotes, long dashes and "…" into plain characters, which some forms and code editors need.
  • Find and replace changes every match at once; Undo reverses the last 30 changes.

"Most used words" lists your most repeated words, leaving out small common ones, with how often each appears. Use it to spot a word you lean on too much, or to check that the key term of an article appears naturally.

Frequently Asked Questions: Word Counter

How do I count words in Google Docs?

Open Tools > Word count, or press Ctrl+Shift+C (Cmd+Shift+C on a Mac). Tick “Display word count while typing” to keep a live count on screen. Select text first to count only that part.

Do spaces count as characters?

Usually, yes. X, text messages, LinkedIn and Instagram count spaces. Some essay and form limits count characters without spaces, which the counter shows separately.

How many words is a 5-minute speech?

About 650–750 words at a comfortable speaking pace of 130–150 words a minute.

How many characters is a text message?

160 characters in standard text, or 70 if the message includes emoji or letters outside the basic Latin alphabet. Longer messages are sent as several parts.

How long should a meta description be?

Aim for 140–155 characters so it isn't cut off in Google's results, and make sure it describes the page accurately.

Is my text saved or uploaded?

It is saved only in this browser so a refresh doesn't lose it, and it is never uploaded. Press Clear to remove it.
