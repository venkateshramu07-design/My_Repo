How to Resize an Image to 20KB in 4 Steps

  1. Choose your photo in the tool above (or several at once). iPhone HEIC photos and PNG screenshots are fine; the result is always a real JPG.
  2. Check the limit: the tool is set to 20 KB. If the form also gives a minimum, such as “10 to 20 KB”, type it in the second box so the file is not too small either.

How to Reduce a Photo to 20 KB Without a Tool

On Windows (Paint): open the photo, choose Resize, select Pixels, untick nothing and type about 600 for the width; save as JPEG and check the size in the file's Properties. If it is still above 20 KB, resize to 450 pixels and save again. Paint does not let you set the JPEG quality, so this takes a few tries.

On a phone: crop the photo to the part you need first (cropping is the biggest saving), then share it to yourself on WhatsApp as a photo, which compresses it to about 100–300 KB; that still needs a second step to reach 20 KB. The tool above does all of this in one go and shows the exact size before you download.

How the Tool Picks the Size

A JPEG's file size depends on two things: how many pixels it has and how strongly it is compressed (the “quality” setting). Most compressors only lower the quality, which is why their 20 KB results look blocky. This tool searches for the highest quality that fits and, if that would fall below about 60%, makes the photo slightly smaller in pixels instead, because a smaller sharp photo looks far better on a form than a large blurry one. It also writes the resolution (dpi) into the file, which some government portals check, and counts 1 KB as 1,000 bytes so the file passes whichever way the portal measures.

Frequently Asked Questions: Resize Image to 20KB

How do I reduce a photo to 20 KB?

Choose it in the tool above with the limit at 20 KB and download. The tool keeps the photo's shape, finds the highest quality that fits under 20 KB and shows the final size, pixels and quality.

What is a 20 KB photo size in pixels?

At good quality, about 400 × 330 pixels for a detailed camera photo and up to 800 × 600 for a simpler image. For a passport-style photo, 276 × 354 pixels (3.5 × 4.5 cm at 200 dpi) fits under 20 KB easily.

How do I make a photo between 20 KB and 50 KB?

Set the maximum to 50 and the minimum to 20, or use the [[post:articles/resize-image-to-50kb|50 KB version of this tool]]. That is the photo range for SSC, IBPS, SBI and RRB forms.

How do I resize a photo to 20 KB in Paint?

Open it in Paint, choose Resize, pick Pixels, set the width to about 600 (keep the aspect ratio ticked), save as JPEG and check the size; repeat with a smaller width until it is under 20 KB.

Why is my 20 KB photo rejected?

Usually because the form also wants exact pixels or a minimum size, or because it counts 1 KB as 1,000 bytes. Set the pixels in the tool if the form gives them; the tool always stays under 20,000 bytes.
