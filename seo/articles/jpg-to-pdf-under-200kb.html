How to Convert JPG to PDF Under 200 KB

  1. Choose the photos of your document pages in the tool above, in page order (select them together).
  2. Keep Auto selected. It keeps colour when it stays clear and switches to black and white when colour would be blurry at 200 KB.
  3. Check the preview and download the PDF.

Each photo is placed on an A4 page with a small margin, turned to landscape if it is wider than tall. For best results, crop each photo to the edges of the paper first.

Tips for Photographing Documents

  • Lay the page flat on a dark table, so its edges stand out, and shoot from directly above.
  • Use daylight from a window instead of the flash, which leaves a bright spot in the middle of the page.
  • Fill the frame with the page and crop away the table afterwards: margins cost as many bytes as text.
  • Use your phone's document scan mode if it has one (Google Drive, the iPhone Notes and Files apps, most camera apps). It straightens the page and whitens the paper, which roughly halves the PDF size.
  • Check every corner is visible. Portals reject certificates with a cut-off seal, signature or serial number.

Where You Need It

National Scholarship Portal schemes typically ask for documents under 200 KB; NEET certificates must be 50–300 KB; the Indian e-Visa passport page 10–300 KB. For a single photo that must stay a JPG instead, use [[post:articles/resize-image-to-200kb|resize image to 200 KB]].

How do I convert a JPG to a PDF under 200 KB?

Choose the JPG in the tool above and download the PDF; the limit is set to 200 KB.

Can I combine several JPGs into one PDF?

Yes. Select them all at once and they become one PDF, in the order you selected them.

How do I make a JPG to PDF under 100 KB?

Type 100 in the size box or tap the 100 KB chip; black and white keeps text sharp at that size.

Will my photo stay in colour?
