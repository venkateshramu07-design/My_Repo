Quick answer on Canadian passport photo size: Canadian passport photos are larger than most: 50 mm wide by 70 mm high, with your face 31–36 mm from chin to crown, against a plain white or light background, taken in the last six months. For printed photos, Canada also asks for the photo studio's name and address and the date on the back, so printed photos must come from a commercial photographer. For online renewal, though, you upload a digital file you can make here: a JPEG of at least 1,200 × 1,800 pixels, 200 KB to 5 MB, with the face 45–50% of the height.

That makes Canada different from the US or UK: a home-printed photo sheet is not enough for a paper application. Use the tool below for an online renewal, to check your pose and framing before you visit a studio, or for other Canadian applications that take digital photos.

Using the Tool for a Canadian Application

  1. Online renewal: choose "A digital photo to upload". The tool saves a 1,200 × 1,800 pixel JPEG within the file-size limits, with the head in the right range. Canada asks for the photographer's details, so ask whoever takes the photo to note their name and address.
  2. Paper application: use a commercial photo studio, as the back of the photos must carry the studio's details. You can use the tool first to check that your hair, glasses and expression will pass.

Common Problems

  • Using the US or Schengen size by mistake: Canadian photos are 50 × 70 mm.
  • Missing studio name, address or date on the back of printed photos.
  • Scanned photos for online renewal: digital photos must be the original camera file.
  • Shadows on the background or face, or glasses with glare.

Frequently Asked Questions: Canadian Passport Photo Size

What if my passport photo is not exactly the right size?

It will usually be rejected. Passport offices check the exact print size and head size, so a Canadian photo must be 50 × 70 mm with the face 31–36 mm from chin to crown. Have it taken by a commercial photographer who knows the Canadian rules.

What are the Canadian passport photo dimensions?

50 mm wide and 70 mm high, with the face from chin to crown 31–36 mm tall, printed by a commercial photographer with their name and the date on the back.

Can I use a 2x2 photo for a Canadian passport?

No. Canadian passport photos must be 50x70 mm, much larger than 2x2 inches (51x51 mm), with the face 31 to 36 mm from chin to crown.

What size is a Canadian passport photo?

50 mm wide by 70 mm high (about 2 × 2 3/4 inches), with the face 31–36 mm from chin to crown.

Can I print my own Canadian passport photos?

Not for a paper application: the photos must show the photo studio's name, address and the date on the back. For online renewal you upload a digital photo instead.

What are the digital photo requirements for online renewal?

A JPEG of at least 1,200 × 1,800 pixels (up to 3,000 × 4,500), 200 KB to 5 MB, with the face 45–50% of the height, taken in the last six months.

Is a Canadian visa photo the same size?

No. Canadian visa and permit applications usually use a 35 × 45 mm photo, like the Schengen size, and are often submitted digitally. Check your application's instructions.
