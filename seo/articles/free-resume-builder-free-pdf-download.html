Quick answer: Many "free" resume builders let you spend an hour writing your resume and then charge you to download it, often through a cheap trial that renews automatically. The builder below never does: fill in your details, pick one of three clean layouts, and download a PDF, a Word file or plain text for free, with no account or trial. It saves your work in your browser as you type. The layouts are single-column with real, selectable text, which is what applicant tracking systems (ATS) read most reliably.

The "download paywall" is one of the most common complaints about online resume tools. Guides to spotting it describe the same pattern: editing is free, but exporting a PDF or Word file isn't; some give away a plain-text file and charge for the PDF; others unlock downloads with a low-cost trial (one well-known site offers 14 days for $2.95) that rolls into a monthly subscription. Career forums often recommend skipping these sites entirely and using a document editor. This builder is the same idea with the formatting done for you.

How to Write Each Section

Header

Your name, the job title you are applying for, email, phone, city and one link (LinkedIn or portfolio). You don't need your full address, date of birth, marital status or a photo in the US, UK, Canada or Australia. Some countries, such as Germany, still expect a photo; add one only if that is the norm where you are applying.

Summary

Two or three lines: who you are, your strongest proof, and what you want next. "Marketing manager with 7 years in B2B software. Grew inbound pipeline 3x and led a team of five." beats a paragraph of adjectives.

Experience

Most recent job first. Under each one, write 2–5 achievements rather than duties. Start each with a verb and add a number wherever you honestly can: how much, how many, how fast, how often.

Weak (a duty)Strong (a result)
Responsible for social mediaGrew Instagram from 4,000 to 31,000 followers in a year
Handled customer complaintsResolved 40+ cases a week and raised satisfaction from 82% to 94%
Worked on the websiteRebuilt 60 landing pages, cutting cost per lead by 38%
Managed the teamLed five people and hired three, with no departures in two years

Education, skills and extras

List degrees and relevant courses with years. In Skills, use the exact words from the job ad where they truly apply ("Google Ads", "Excel", "Python"), because ATS and recruiters search for them. Use the extra section for certifications, languages, awards or volunteering.

Making Your Resume Easy for ATS to Read

  • Use a single column, standard headings (Experience, Education, Skills) and real text, all of which these templates do. Avoid putting key information in images, text boxes or tables.
  • Send the file type they ask for. PDF keeps your layout exactly; some systems prefer Word, so use the .doc download; for online forms that strip formatting, paste from the plain-text version.
  • Match the job ad's language for skills and job titles where it is honest to do so.
  • Keep it to one page early in your career and two pages at most for most roles.
  • Name the file clearly: the builder saves it as First-Last-resume.pdf.

How to Download the PDF

Press "Download PDF" and your device's print window opens with only the resume in it. Choose Save as PDF as the printer (on iPhone, tap Share then Save to Files; on Android, choose "Save as PDF" at the top). This uses your device's own PDF engine, so the text stays sharp and selectable. Choose US Letter or A4 to match the country you are applying in.

Your Data Stays With You

Everything you type is saved in this browser only, so you can close the page and continue later on the same device. Use "Save a backup" to keep a copy of your details as a file, which you can load on another device or use to make versions for different jobs. "Clear everything" deletes it from the browser.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a resume builder that is actually free to download?

Yes. This builder lets you download your resume as PDF, Word or plain text free, without an account, trial or watermark. Many other "free" builders charge at the download step, so check before you start typing.

Is PDF or Word better for a resume?

PDF is best in most cases because the layout can't shift. Send Word only if the employer asks for it. For online application forms, paste from the plain-text version.

Are these resume templates ATS-friendly?

Yes. They are single-column, use standard section headings and produce real, selectable text with no images or text boxes, which ATS software reads reliably.

Should I put a photo on my resume?

Not in the US, UK, Canada, Ireland or Australia, where it is generally discouraged. In parts of Europe, Asia and the Middle East a photo is still common; follow the norm for the country and employer.

Where is my resume saved?

Only in your browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Save a backup file if you want to keep a copy or move it to another device.
