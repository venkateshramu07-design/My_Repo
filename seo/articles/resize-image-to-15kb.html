Photo or Signature at 15 KB With Exact Pixels

Many bank and exam forms give exact pixels as well as KB. For an IBPS signature (140 × 60 pixels, 10–20 KB), choose “Set width and height in pixels”, type 140 and 60, set the minimum to 10 and the maximum to 20. At only 8,400 pixels, a clean signature is usually smaller than 10 KB even at the highest quality, so the tool adds an invisible blank block inside the file to reach 10 KB. In our test it produced a 10.1 KB file at 140 × 60 pixels and 95% quality, which the portal accepts.

How the Tool Picks the Size

A JPEG's file size depends on two things: how many pixels it has and how strongly it is compressed (the “quality” setting). Most compressors only lower the quality, which is why their 20 KB results look blocky. This tool searches for the highest quality that fits and, if that would fall below about 60%, makes the photo slightly smaller in pixels instead, because a smaller sharp photo looks far better on a form than a large blurry one. It also writes the resolution (dpi) into the file, which some government portals check, and counts 1 KB as 1,000 bytes so the file passes whichever way the portal measures.

Frequently Asked Questions: Resize Image to 15KB

How do I make a photo 15 KB?

Choose the photo in the tool above, keep the limit at 15 KB and download. If the form also fixes the pixels, select “Set width and height in pixels” and enter them first.

What is the size of a 15 KB photo in pixels?

There is no fixed answer, because detail compresses differently, but at good quality it is roughly 340 × 280 pixels for a camera photo and up to 600 × 450 for a simpler image.

Is 15 KB good for a signature?

Yes. It is in the middle of the common 10–20 KB signature range, so it passes forms that measure KB either as 1,000 or 1,024 bytes.

How do I resize a photo to between 10 and 20 KB?

Set the maximum to 20 and the minimum to 10 in the tool. It aims as close to 20 KB as it can for sharpness and pads the file if the picture is too simple to reach 10 KB.

Does resizing to 15 KB reduce the quality?

Some, unavoidably, but the tool keeps JPEG quality at 60% or higher by reducing the pixel size a little instead, which looks much better than a heavily compressed full-size photo.
