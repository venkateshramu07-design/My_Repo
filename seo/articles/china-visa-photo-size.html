Quick answer: A China visa photo is 33 mm wide by 48 mm high, with the head 28–33 mm from chin to crown and 15–22 mm wide, in colour, on a plain white background, taken in the last six months. For the online visa form you upload a digital JPEG of 354 × 472 to 420 × 560 pixels and 40–120 KB. Make either version free below: the tool saves the digital file at 354 × 472 pixels inside the KB range, or a printable 4 × 6 inch sheet.

China's online visa form checks the photo automatically and rejects files with the wrong pixel size, the wrong file size or a background that isn't white, which is why many applicants get stuck at the upload step.

How to Make It

  1. Take the photo against a white wall with even light, glasses off, hair away from your face and ears.
  2. Add it to the tool with "China: visa" selected and fit your face to the oval.
  3. For the online form choose "A digital photo to upload": the file is 354 × 472 px and between 40 and 120 KB.
  4. For a paper application, print the sheet at actual size and cut out the photos.

Frequently Asked Questions: China Visa Photo Size

What if my visa photo is not the exact size?

The visa centre or online system can reject it and ask for a new one, which delays the application. The China visa photo must be 33 × 48 mm (354–420 × 472–560 pixels online); the photo maker on this page crops it exactly.

What is the China visa photo size requirement?

A colour photo 33 mm wide and 48 mm high on a white background, taken in the last six months, with the head 28–33 mm tall; online forms ask for a digital photo of 354–420 × 472–560 pixels between 40 and 120 KB.

Is the China visa photo the same as a US passport photo?

No. A China visa photo is 33x48 mm, taller and narrower than the US 2x2 inch passport photo, so a US passport photo will be rejected. Make the correct size with the tool above.

What size is a China visa photo?

33 × 48 mm printed, or a digital JPEG of 354 × 472 to 420 × 560 pixels and 40–120 KB.

Why does the online form reject my photo?

Usually because the file is outside 40–120 KB, the pixel size is wrong, or the background isn't white enough. The tool fixes the first two and checks the background.

Can I wear glasses in a China visa photo?

It's best not to: many visa centres reject photos with glasses even without glare.

Is the China visa photo the same as a passport photo?

No. It is narrower and taller (33 × 48 mm) than the 35 × 45 mm standard and the US 2 × 2 inch size.
