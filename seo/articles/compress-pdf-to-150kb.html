How to Compress a PDF to 150KB in 4 Steps

  1. Choose your PDF in the tool above. You can also choose photos of the pages (JPG or PNG); they become one PDF, one photo per A4 page.

Getting a Clean Scan in the First Place

The smaller the limit, the more the original scan matters. Photograph the page flat, in daylight, from directly above, and crop to the paper's edges. A phone's “document scan” mode (in Google Drive, the iPhone Notes and Files apps, or most camera apps) straightens the page and whitens the background, which can halve the compressed size. Then add the result to the tool.

What Happens to My PDF When It Is Compressed?

The tool first tries a lossless squeeze: it rebuilds the PDF with compressed internal structures, which keeps text selectable and changes nothing you can see. That is often enough for PDFs made on a computer. If the file is still too big, usually because it is a scan or contains photos, it redraws every page as an image: in colour as a JPEG, using the highest resolution and quality that fit, or as a pure black-and-white image, which is how fax and document scanners store text and is often ten times smaller than colour. In Auto mode the tool keeps colour when it stays clear and uses black and white when colour pages would be blurry. The page size stays the same (A4 stays A4), it prints the same, and the preview shows the first pages so you can check every line before you download. Nothing is uploaded: the PDF is read and rebuilt inside your browser by the open-source PDF.js and pdf-lib libraries.

Frequently Asked Questions: Compress PDF to 150KB

How do I compress a PDF to 150 KB?

Choose it in the tool above with the limit at 150 KB and download.

Is 150 KB enough for a scanned certificate?

Yes. A single certificate page fits at good quality in colour, and several pages fit in black and white.

How do I compress a PDF to between 100 and 150 KB?

Set the tool to 150 KB. It aims close to the limit for the best quality, so the result is usually between 140 and 150 KB.

Can I compress password-protected PDFs?

Not directly. Open the PDF with its password, save or print it to a new PDF without the password, then compress that copy.

Does compressing reduce the page size?

No. A4 pages stay A4 and print the same size; only the file gets smaller.
