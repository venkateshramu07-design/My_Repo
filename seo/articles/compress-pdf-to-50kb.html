Where You Need a PDF Under 50 KB

How Many Pages Fit in 50 KB?

From our tests, a typed or printed A4 page in pure black and white takes about 10–16 KB at 150 dpi, so 3 to 4 pages fit in 50 KB with sharp text. Dense pages (small print on every line) take more; mostly blank pages take less. In colour, one A4 page needs around 50 KB even at a low resolution. What Happens to My PDF When It Is Compressed?

The tool first tries a lossless squeeze: it rebuilds the PDF with compressed internal structures, which keeps text selectable and changes nothing you can see. That is often enough for PDFs made on a computer. If the file is still too big, usually because it is a scan or contains photos, it redraws every page as an image: in colour as a JPEG, using the highest resolution and quality that fit, or as a pure black-and-white image, which is how fax and document scanners store text and is often ten times smaller than colour. In Auto mode the tool keeps colour when it stays clear and uses black and white when colour pages would be blurry. The page size stays the same (A4 stays A4), it prints the same, and the preview shows the first pages so you can check every line before you download. Nothing is uploaded: the PDF is read and rebuilt inside your browser by the open-source PDF.js and pdf-lib libraries.

Frequently Asked Questions: Compress PDF to 50KB

How do I compress a PDF to 50 KB?

Choose it in the tool above, select Black and white and download. The result line shows the final size and how it was saved.

Can I compress a PDF to 50 KB without losing quality?

For a typed or computer-made PDF, sometimes: the tool tries a lossless squeeze first. For scans, some loss is unavoidable, but pure black and white keeps text sharp at 150 dpi.

Why is my PDF still more than 50 KB?

It probably has many pages or photos. Try Black and white, remove pages you do not need, or crop the scan to the paper's edges before making the PDF.

How do I convert a JPG to a PDF under 50 KB?

Choose the JPG in the tool above instead of a PDF; it becomes a one-page PDF under the size you set. Black and white works best for documents.

Is it safe to compress a certificate online?

With this tool, yes: the PDF is processed in your browser and never uploaded. Many other sites send your file to their servers.
