Quick answer on US passport photo requirements: A US passport photo must be 2 × 2 inches (51 × 51 mm), in colour, taken in the last six months, against a plain white or off-white background, with your head measuring 1 to 1 3/8 inches (25–35 mm) from the bottom of your chin to the top of your head. Glasses are not allowed. Make one free below: add a photo, fit your face to the oval, and print the 4 × 6 inch sheet of six photos at any pharmacy or supermarket photo kiosk.

The State Department rejects a large number of applications because of the photo, which delays the passport by weeks. Most rejections come down to a handful of things: shadows, glasses, a head that is too small, or a photo that was edited. All of them are easy to avoid at home.

Step by Step at Home

  1. Stand about two feet in front of a plain white or off-white wall, facing a window.
  2. Take off your glasses and anything on your head. Push hair away from your eyes.
  3. Have someone take the photo from about four to five feet away, with the phone at your eye level and the flash off.
  4. Add the photo to the tool, keep "United States: passport" selected, and drag and zoom until your chin sits on the bottom of the oval and the top of your head on the top.
  5. Choose "Printed photos" and download the sheet: six 2 × 2 inch photos on one 4 × 6 inch print.
  6. Order a standard 4 × 6 print at actual size (no cropping or "fit to page"), cut out two photos, and attach one to your DS-11 or DS-82 form as instructed.

Common Reasons US Passport Photos Are Rejected

  • Glasses. Since 2016 they are not allowed, even with no glare.
  • Shadows on the face or on the wall behind you.
  • Head too small, usually because the photo was taken from too far away and not cropped. The oval in the tool sets the size for you.
  • Background not white, such as a cream or grey wall that looks darker in the photo.
  • Old photos that no longer match your current appearance.
  • Edited photos, including beauty filters and phone "portrait" effects.

Online Renewal

If you renew online through MyTravelGov, you upload a digital photo instead of mailing prints. It must meet the same rules (taken by someone else, white background, no glasses). Choose "A digital photo to upload" for a square 600 × 600 pixel file, and check the upload instructions in your MyTravelGov account for the current file limits before you submit.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the US passport photo requirements for infants?

The same 2 × 2 inch size and plain white background, with the baby facing the camera. Eyes should be open if possible (newborns are allowed some leeway), no hands or toys in the shot, and if someone supports the baby, they must not be visible: lay the baby on a white sheet instead.

Do I need to bring a 2x2 picture for my passport?

Yes for a paper application: you must include one 2x2 inch colour photo taken in the last six months. If you renew online, you upload a digital photo instead.

What if my passport photo is not exactly 2x2?

It will probably be rejected. The State Department requires exactly 2x2 inches (51x51 mm) with the head 1 to 1 3/8 inches from chin to top. Print the sheet from this tool at actual size, not "fit to page".

Can I print a US passport photo at home or at a kiosk?

Yes. The State Department accepts photos you take and print yourself if they meet the requirements. A kiosk print of the 4 × 6 inch sheet on photo paper is usually the cheapest option.

Can I smile in a US passport photo?

A natural, closed-mouth smile is acceptable; a neutral expression is safest. Both eyes must be open.

Can I wear glasses in my passport photo?

No. Glasses have not been allowed in US passport photos since November 2016, except in rare medical cases with a signed statement from a doctor.

Are a US passport photo and a US visa photo the same?

The size is the same (2 × 2 inches), but visa applications usually need a digital file. See the [[post:articles/us-visa-photo-600x600|US visa and DV lottery photo guide]].
