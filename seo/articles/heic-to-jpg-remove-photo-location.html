Convert HEIC to JPG

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is the format iPhones and iPads use by default. It saves space, roughly half the size of a JPG at the same quality, but outside Apple's world it often won't open. Add your HEIC photos above, choose JPG, and press Convert. On iPhone and Mac the photo is opened directly; on other devices the tool downloads a small decoder the first time.

To stop the problem at the source, you can change two iPhone settings:

  • Settings > Camera > Formats > Most Compatible makes the camera save new photos as JPG.
  • Settings > Photos > Transfer to Mac or PC > Automatic converts photos to JPG when you copy them to a computer by cable.

Both only affect photos taken or transferred after you change them, so this converter is still the quickest fix for the ones you already have.

Remove Location and Other Hidden Data

Photos carry EXIF data: the camera or phone model, the date and time, and often the latitude and longitude where they were taken. Anyone who receives the original file can read it, and privacy guides warn that GPS coordinates in a photo taken at home can be turned into a street address in seconds. Some apps and social networks strip it when you post, but email, messaging as a "file", cloud links and many websites pass the original on unchanged.

When you add a photo, the tool reads its EXIF data and shows a red "Contains GPS location" warning, with a map link, if a location is present. The converted copy is redrawn from the pixels only, so all of that data is gone: no location, no device, no date. That also removes the hidden preview thumbnail that some cameras embed, which can still show parts of a photo you cropped out.

To stop recording locations in future, turn off Location for the Camera app (iPhone: Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services > Camera; Android: in the Camera app's settings).

JPG, WebP or PNG?

  • JPG opens everywhere and suits photos. Choose it when in doubt.
  • WebP is about 25–35% smaller than JPG at similar quality and works in all modern browsers. It is good for websites, but some forms and older programs won't accept it.
  • PNG keeps every pixel exactly and supports transparency, but photos come out much larger. Use it for screenshots, logos and graphics.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert HEIC to JPG on Windows for free?

Open this page in Chrome or Edge, add your HEIC photos, choose JPG and press Convert. The conversion happens on your PC, nothing is uploaded, and you can download the photos individually or as a zip.

How can I reduce a photo to under 100 KB?

Choose "Make it smaller than…", enter 100, optionally set the longest side to around 800–1200 pixels, and convert. The tool picks the best quality that fits under 100 KB.

Does sending a photo share my location?

It can. Original photo files often contain GPS coordinates. Some apps remove them when you post, but files sent by email, cloud links or "send as document" usually keep them. Converting the photo here removes the location.

Will converting reduce the quality?

At quality 85–95, most people can't see the difference. Lower settings or strict size limits trade quality for size; PNG keeps full quality but makes bigger files.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Photos are processed in your browser and never leave your device. The only download is the HEIC decoder code, the first time you convert a HEIC photo on a non-Apple device.
