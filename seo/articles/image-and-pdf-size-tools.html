How to Reduce Image Size in KB Without Losing Quality

  1. Crop first. Cutting away background you do not need is the biggest saving and costs no quality.
  2. Use the right pixel size. A form shows your photo at a few hundred pixels; a 4,000-pixel original wastes space. The tool reduces pixels before quality.
  3. Use black and white for signatures and text. It removes grey paper texture, which is most of the file.
  4. Save as JPG, not PNG, for photos. PNG is lossless and often ten times bigger.
  5. Stay a little under the limit. Forms count KB differently; the tools keep files under the limit in bytes so they pass either way.
