Quick answer on kb to mb: Divide by 1,024 to turn KB into MB the way Windows and most upload forms count (500 KB = 0.488 MB), or by 1,000 the way phones and Macs count (500 KB = 0.5 MB). The converter below shows both at once. To stay safe on a form that says “under 200 KB”, keep your file under 195 KB as Windows shows it.

Two systems are in everyday use. The international standard (SI) and storage makers use 1 KB = 1,000 bytes; Windows and many programmers use 1 KB = 1,024 bytes, which the standards body calls a kibibyte (KiB). The difference is small for kilobytes and grows with each step: 2.4% for KB, 4.9% for MB, 7.4% for GB.

Which One Does My Upload Form Use?

Forms rarely say. Many websites check the file in bytes and compare it with 200 × 1,024 = 204,800 bytes for “200 KB”; others use 200,000. A file Windows shows as 199 KB is 203,776 bytes, which passes the first and fails the second. The safe rule is to stay under the limit × 1,000 bytes, which is what our [[post:articles/image-and-pdf-size-tools|resize and compress tools]] do automatically.

Why My Phone and Laptop Show Different Sizes

The same file can show as 1.05 MB on an iPhone or Mac and 1,000 KB (0.98 MB) on Windows. Nothing changed: Apple's systems count 1 MB as 1,000,000 bytes and Windows counts 1,048,576. The same thing explains why a new 128 GB phone or memory card shows only about 119 GB in Windows: the maker counts in thousands, Windows in multiples of 1,024. When an upload form rejects a file that “looks” small enough, check the exact size in bytes, shown in brackets in Windows Properties.

How to Check a File's Size

  • Windows: right-click the file, choose Properties and read “Size” (in 1,024-based KB) and the exact bytes in brackets.
  • Mac: select the file and press Command-I. Finder uses 1,000-based KB.
  • Android: open the Files app, tap and hold the file, then the ⓘ details icon.
  • iPhone: in the Files app, touch and hold the file and choose Get Info. For photos, open the photo and swipe up.

Frequently Asked Questions: KB to MB

How many KB are in 1 MB?

1,024 KB in the binary system (Windows, most forms) or 1,000 KB in the decimal system (phones, Macs, storage makers).

How do I convert KB to MB?

Divide by 1,024 (binary) or 1,000 (decimal). 300 KB is 0.293 MB or 0.3 MB.

How do I convert MB to KB?

Multiply by 1,024 or 1,000. 2 MB is 2,048 KB or 2,000 KB.

Is 500 KB bigger than 1 MB?

No. 500 KB is about half of 1 MB in either system.

What is a KiB?

A kibibyte, exactly 1,024 bytes. The standards say KB should mean 1,000 bytes and KiB 1,024, but Windows still labels KiB as KB.
