Good to know

Are these tools really free?

Yes. Every tool and calculator is free to use as often as you like, with no account, trial or watermark.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. PDFs, photos and documents are opened and processed inside your browser on your own device, and nothing is sent to Blog Digest or anyone else.

Do the calculators use current rates?

Each calculator page shows the date and source of its figures, such as FY 2026-27 tax slabs and October-December 2026 savings rates, and you can change the rates yourself.
