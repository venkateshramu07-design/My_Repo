This policy explains what information Blog Digest (randomthoughtsever.blogspot.com) collects when you visit, how it is used, and the choices you have. We collect as little as we can.

Information You Give Us

  • Email newsletter. If you subscribe, your email address is stored by our email provider, Brevo, and used only to send you new articles. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing removes you from the list.
  • Contact messages and comments. If you contact us or leave a comment, we receive the details you provide, such as your name and email address, and use them only to reply or to publish your comment.

Calculators and Tools

Our calculators run entirely in your browser. What you type is not sent to us or stored on any server. A few tools, such as the digital legacy checklist and your reading history, save your choices in your own browser's local storage so they are there when you return. You can clear this at any time in your browser settings.

Information Collected Automatically

Like most websites, Blog Digest is hosted on a platform (Google's Blogger) that records standard technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you view and the time of your visit. We use Google Analytics to understand, in aggregate, which articles are read and how visitors find us. This helps us decide what to write and improve. We do not use this information to identify you personally.

Advertising and Cookies

Blog Digest is supported by advertising from Google AdSense. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • To learn more, see how Google uses cookies in advertising and how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads and non-essential cookies are used. You can change your choice at any time using the privacy and cookie settings link shown on the page, or by clearing cookies in your browser.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct or delete it, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. This includes rights under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation and California privacy law. We do not sell personal information. To make a request, use our contact page. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Blog Digest is written for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Links to Other Websites

Our articles link to official sources and other websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, so please read their policies when you visit them.
