Rates in force in October 2026. EU countries, Serbia, Türkiye and the US federal rate: Eurostat, July 2026 (monthly). Others: official rates converted to a month of full-time work (40 hours a week; Australia 38; daily rates × 26 days, Mexico × 30.4). Denmark, Italy, Austria, Finland, Sweden, Norway and Switzerland have no national statutory minimum wage; pay floors are set by collective agreements.
