Figures are not adjusted for inflation. The cash-out comparison assumes the cash is invested with no tax on its growth, which flatters cashing out; in practice it would usually be taxed. As a guide, early withdrawals cost a 10% penalty plus income tax in the US, 35% to 65% for temporary residents leaving Australia, and up to 55% for unauthorised payments from UK pensions before age 55.
