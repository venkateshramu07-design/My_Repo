Limits apply per person, per licensed bank. Joint accounts and, in the US and Canada, some retirement and trust accounts can be insured separately, so your real cover may be higher. App balances may be safeguarded, or covered through a partner bank in some cases, but neither is the same as a fixed government guarantee. This is a guide, not a ruling on your accounts.
