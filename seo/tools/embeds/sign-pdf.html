Sign a PDF (free, no account)
Open a PDF, draw, type or upload your signature, place it on the page, add the date or any text, and download the signed copy. The document never leaves your device, and there is no limit on how many you sign.
Sign with your finger, stylus or mouse
A photo of your signature on white paper works; the white background is removed.
Add to the page
Items go on the page you are looking at. Drag to move, pull the corner to resize, tap × to remove.
Your PDF and signature stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded or stored unless you tick “Remember”, which keeps the signature in this browser only.
