BMI calculator (with Asian cut-offs)

Body mass index is a quick screening measure, not a diagnosis. South Asians tend to develop type 2 diabetes at lower BMIs, so lower cut-offs are often used in India.

WHO: under 18.5 underweight, 18.5 to 24.9 normal, 25 to 29.9 overweight, 30 and above obese. Asian / Indian cut-offs used by Indian consensus guidelines: 18 to 22.9 normal, 23 to 24.9 overweight, 25 and above obese. BMI doesn't distinguish muscle from fat; waist size matters too.
