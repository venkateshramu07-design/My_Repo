Before you submit: quick checklist
  • Taken in the last 6 months, in colour, sharp and evenly lit.
  • Looking straight at the camera, neutral expression, mouth closed, eyes open.
  • No glasses for US photos (other countries allow them only without glare or tint).
  • No hat or head covering, except for religious or medical reasons.
  • Plain light background with no shadows, objects or other people.
  • Not edited: no filters, beauty mode or retouching.
