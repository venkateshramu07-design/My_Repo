Mask Aadhaar, PAN or any ID before you share it
Cover your Aadhaar number, date of birth, address, QR code or signature with solid boxes, add a "for this purpose only" watermark, and save a safe copy as JPG or PDF. The file is opened and changed on your device only.

Drag across anything you want hidden: a black box covers it. For an Aadhaar card, cover the first 8 digits of the number (keep the last 4), plus the QR code if you don't want your details read from it.

Your document never leaves this device. Saved pages are flattened into images, so hidden numbers cannot be uncovered or copied from the file.
