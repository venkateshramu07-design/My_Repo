Meeting planner across time zones
Add the cities of everyone in the meeting. Green hours are normal working hours (9 am to 6 pm) in each place; columns where everyone is green are the best times. Tap an hour to get the time in every city, ready to paste into an invite or add to your calendar. Daylight saving is handled for the date you pick.
Runs in your browser using your device’s time-zone database. Nothing is sent anywhere.
