A widely used guideline, sometimes called the 20/4/10 rule, suggests putting at least 20% down, borrowing for no more than four years, and keeping car costs to about 10% of gross income; 15% of take-home pay is a similar test. A negative trade-in figure means you owe more than the old car is worth, and that amount is added to the new loan.
