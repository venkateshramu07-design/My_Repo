Do I need to declare my cash?
Pick where you fly from and to, and how much you carry. You get the rule on both ends of the trip: whether to declare, how, and any limit on local currency.

Add up everything for the whole family or group travelling together: banknotes in every currency, travellers' cheques and money orders. Most countries count them together.
