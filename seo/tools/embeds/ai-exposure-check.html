A rough guide, not a prediction. It weights routine writing, data and template work as highly exposed, routine questions as partly exposed, and judgement, physical and human-contact work as least exposed, in line with research on which tasks current AI tools perform well. Exposure means tools can change how the job is done; it does not mean the job will disappear.
