## Health and law
- VA National Center for PTSD (updated 5 May 2026): trauma-focused therapies with the most research support: CPT, EMDR, PE; usually 8-16 sessions; medicines: paroxetine, sertraline, venlafaxine.
- NIMH ADHD (reviewed Aug 2026): standard treatments medication and psychosocial interventions such as CBT; newer: neurofeedback, cognitive training, mindfulness, neuromodulation.
- CDC MMWR 73(40), 10 Oct 2024 (Staley et al.): 15.5 million US adults (6.0%) current ADHD diagnosis; 55.9% diagnosed at 18+; 36.5% no treatment in past 12 months; survey Oct-Nov 2023, n=7,046.
- USPTO "What is a trademark" (updated 30 Nov 2023): TM goods, SM services, usable without filing; ® only after registration and only for the listed goods/services.
- 15 U.S.C. 1111: notice by "Registered in U.S. Patent and Trademark Office", "Reg. U.S. Pat. & Tm. Off." or ®; without notice no profits/damages unless actual notice.
- Cornell LII Wex: contract (mutual assent, consideration, capacity, legality; UCC governs sale of goods); statute of frauds (land; not performable within one year; UCC 2-201 goods $500+); tort (intentional, negligent, strict liability); negligence (duty, breach, cause-in-fact, proximate cause, harm; Hand formula B < PL); strict liability (animals, abnormally dangerous activities, products); intentional torts (battery, assault, false imprisonment, trespass to land, trespass to chattels, IIED).
