Quick answer: The median occupational therapist in the United States earns $100,330 a year, or $48.24 an hour, according to the Bureau of Labor Statistics (May 2025 survey, the latest, published in 2026). The lowest-paid 10% earn under $71,690; the highest-paid 10% earn over $131,950. By state, the median runs from $124,510 in California to $83,200 in North Dakota.

What It Takes to Earn This Salary

  • A graduate degree in occupational therapy from an accredited program: a master's (the BLS's typical entry level) or an entry-level clinical doctorate (OTD). Both lead to the same licence.
  • Fieldwork: supervised clinical placements are part of every accredited program.
  • The national certification exam, after which you can use the title OTR (Occupational Therapist, Registered).
  • A state licence: all states require one, with continuing education to renew.

How to Earn More

  • Home health and skilled nursing pay the most, about $8,000 a year more at the median than outpatient therapy offices and $19,000 more than schools.
  • Per-visit and per-diem home-health work pays well per hour but has unpaid travel time; compare offers on an hourly-equivalent basis.
  • Specialty certification (for example in hand therapy, pediatrics or low vision) supports higher pay in specialist clinics.
  • Look across a state line. The median gap between neighbouring states is often $10,000 or more.
  • Price the whole offer: productivity targets, continuing-education allowance, licence fees and mileage reimbursement differ a lot between employers.

Job Outlook to 2035

The BLS projects occupational therapist jobs to grow 15% from 2025 to 2035, "much faster than average": from 169,600 to about 194,800 jobs, with about 10,000 openings a year.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does an occupational therapist make an hour?

The national median is $48.24 an hour; half earn between $39.67 and $56.09.

What is the starting salary for an occupational therapist?

The BLS doesn't track pay by experience. The 10th percentile, $71,690, is the usual guide to a first job.

Which state pays occupational therapists the most?

California ($124,510), then Oregon ($112,310) and Nevada ($110,520).

How much do occupational therapists make in Texas?

The Texas median is $106,140 ($51.03 an hour).

How much do occupational therapists make in California?

The California median is $124,510 ($59.86 an hour), the highest in the country.

How much do occupational therapists make in Florida?

The Florida median is $99,360 ($47.77 an hour).

Do occupational therapists make six figures?

At the national median, just: $100,330. In the higher-paying states and in home health and skilled nursing, most do.

Is an OTD paid more than a master's?

Not usually for the same clinical job. Employers pay for the licence and setting; the doctorate helps mainly for teaching, research and leadership roles.
