# Rules for new articles (set by the owner, 3 and 10 November 2026)

## Picking the topic
1. Low keyword difficulty (under 30 of 100).
2. High search volume. For the November batch 2: at least 60,000 US searches a month.
3. High CPC.
4. Attractive and specific, never general ("what is finance" is out).
5. Validated before it is proposed: Google's live top 10 must already contain smaller or weaker sites
   (local businesses, small firm blogs, forums, video). Topics owned by the big publishers, banks or agencies are dropped.
6. No brand searches (bank, lender, insurer or app names).
7. US market first.

## Writing the article
8. Editorial standard: it must stand next to a publication with a 20-year desk. Every figure comes from the primary
   source (agency, statute, official data file) and carries its date; every worked example is recalculated by code.
9. No filler. The answer comes first. No scene-setting intros, no restating the question, no padding to reach a length.
10. Leave no space for a competitor: cover every question the current top results and Google's "People also ask"
    cover, plus what they miss (current-year figures, the table they lack, the worked example). The reader should not
    need a second page. Cite official sources only; do not send readers to competing publishers.

## Cover
11. No empty large tile. Each cover is illustrated, attractive, not too bright, and not identical to the others.
