Frequently Asked Questions: Career Guide

How do I avoid burnout at work?

Set clear limits on your working hours, take real breaks, prioritise the tasks that matter, and talk to your manager early if your workload is unsustainable. The World Health Organization treats burnout as a result of chronic workplace stress, so the fix is partly in how work is organised, not just in you.

When should I ask for a raise?

Good moments are after a clear success, when your responsibilities have grown, or ahead of the annual review when budgets are set. Come with evidence of your results and what similar roles pay.

How do I know it is time to change jobs?

Signs include no room to grow, constant stress, values that no longer match the company's, and pay well below the market. Plan the move before you resign where you can.

What skills matter most for career growth?

Clear communication, reliability, the ability to learn quickly and to work well with others matter in almost every field, alongside the technical skills of your job.
