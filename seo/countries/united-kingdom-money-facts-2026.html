Minimum Wage in the United Kingdom (2026)

The legal minimum wage in the United Kingdom is £12.71/hour. For a month of full-time work that is about $2,945 at current exchange rates, 3rd highest of 38 countries in our comparison. National Living Wage for 21+ from 1 April 2026.

Income Tax and Take-Home Pay in the United Kingdom

A single worker on the average wage in the United Kingdom (about $82,000 at purchasing-power parity) pays 23.1% of gross pay in income tax and employee social security, so they keep 77%. That is 12th lowest of 38 countries, against an OECD average of 25.1%.

Income tax and employee social security take 23.1% of pay at the average wage, 18.6% at two-thirds of it and 30.7% at 1.67 times it. Including employer contributions, the total tax wedge is 32.4% of labour costs.

Pay Rises in the United Kingdom: 2027 Forecast

Employers in the United Kingdom expect to raise salaries by 3.3% in 2027 (median of the major pay surveys), after 3.4% in 2026. With inflation at 3.5% (Sep), that is a real cut of about 0.2 points unless inflation falls.

Mortgage Rates in the United Kingdom (2026)

The typical mortgage rate in the United Kingdom is 6% (5-year fixed, 5 Oct 2026), 6th highest of 31 countries.

Moneyfacts average, the highest since 2023; 2-year fixes average 5.98%. Per £100,000 over 25 years: £644 a month.

Electricity Prices in the United Kingdom

Households in the United Kingdom pay about 26.32p per kWh of electricity (about $0.35), 6th most expensive of 33 countries. Basis: Ofgem price cap, Oct–Dec 2026.

Unit rate under the price cap for England, Scotland and Wales, plus a 54.83p daily standing charge. Gas: 7.97p per kWh.

Petrol Prices in the United Kingdom Since the Iran War

Petrol in the United Kingdom costs 174.6p/L (week of 19 October 2026), up 32.5% from 131.7p/L in the week before the Iran war began. It peaked at 175.3p/L (12 Oct). At about $2.33 a litre it is 8th most expensive of 29 countries, and the rise is 9th largest of 29 countries.

Retirement Age in the United Kingdom

The state pension age in the United Kingdom is 66, rising to 67 for people reaching it in 2026; the earliest you can claim is No early state pension. Future change: 67 by March 2028; 68 planned for 2044–46. The rise to 67 began in May 2026 for people born on or after 6 April 1960. Private pensions can be accessed from 55 (57 from 2028).

How Much the State Pension Pays in the United Kingdom

A full-career average earner in the United Kingdom can expect a mandatory pension worth about 54.2% of their previous net pay, 18th least generous of 50 countries (OECD average 63.2%). Low earner (half the average wage): 76%. High earner (twice the average): 40%.

How Much of Your Money Is Protected in the United Kingdom

Bank deposits in the United Kingdom are protected up to £120,000 per depositor, per bank (about $160,400), under the FSCS scheme. From 1 December 2025; temporary high balances up to £1.4m. Money in payment apps and e-money wallets is usually not covered.

Inheritance Tax in the United Kingdom

The United Kingdom has estate tax. Children can receive £325,000 + £175,000 for a home left to children tax-free, and the top rate for children is 40%, 4th highest of 28 countries. 40% above the allowances, which pass between spouses (up to £1 million for a couple); unused pensions taxable from April 2027.

Frequently Asked Questions: UK Minimum Wage 2026

What is the minimum wage in the United Kingdom in 2026?
