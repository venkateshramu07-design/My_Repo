Minimum Wage in Germany (2026)

The legal minimum wage in Germany is €2,343/month. For a month of full-time work that is about $2,623 at current exchange rates, 5th highest of 38 countries in our comparison. €13.90 an hour from January 2026.

Income Tax and Take-Home Pay in Germany

A single worker on the average wage in Germany (about $94,000 at purchasing-power parity) pays 38.7% of gross pay in income tax and employee social security, so they keep 61%. That is 2nd highest of 38 countries, against an OECD average of 25.1%.

Income tax and employee social security take 38.7% of pay at the average wage, 34.1% at two-thirds of it and 42.3% at 1.67 times it. Including employer contributions, the total tax wedge is 49.3% of labour costs.

Pay Rises in Germany: 2027 Forecast

Employers in Germany expect to raise salaries by 3.1% in 2027 (median of the major pay surveys), after 3.2% in 2026. With inflation at 3.3% (Sep), that is a real cut of about 0.2 points unless inflation falls.

Mortgage Rates in Germany (2026)

The typical mortgage rate in Germany is 4% (new home loans, August 2026), 11th highest of 31 countries; the euro area average is 3.6%.

Up 0.23 points since February 2026 (3.77%). Monthly payment per €100,000 over 25 years: €528.

Electricity Prices in Germany

Households in Germany pay about €0.362 per kWh of electricity (about $0.41), 2nd most expensive of 33 countries; the EU average is €0.283. Change: −6.5% vs late 2025.

Household gas: €0.116 per kWh. Typical household using 2,500–5,000 kWh a year, all taxes included, January–June 2026.

Petrol Prices in Germany Since the Iran War

Petrol in Germany costs €2.18/L (week of 19 October 2026), up 19.5% from €1.82/L in the week before the Iran war began. It peaked at €2.36/L (14 Sep). At about $2.44 a litre it is 6th most expensive of 29 countries, and the rise is 6th smallest of 29 countries.

Retirement Age in Germany

The state pension age in Germany is 66 y 4 m for people reaching it in 2026; the earliest you can claim is 63 (with 35 years, reduced). Future change: 67 by 2031. 66 y 4 m applies to people born in 1960; the age rises two months per birth year to 67 for those born in 1964 or later.

How Much the State Pension Pays in Germany

A full-career average earner in Germany can expect a mandatory pension worth about 53.3% of their previous net pay, 17th least generous of 50 countries (OECD average 63.2%). Low earner (half the average wage): 58%. High earner (twice the average): 39%. Including common voluntary private pensions, an average earner would get 68%.

How Much of Your Money Is Protected in Germany

Bank deposits in Germany are protected up to €100,000 per depositor, per bank (about $111,900), the limit set by EU law for every member state. Harmonised by EU law; local-currency equivalent outside the euro. Money in payment apps and e-money wallets is usually not covered.

Inheritance Tax in Germany

Germany has inheritance tax. Children can receive €400,000 per child (€500,000 spouse) tax-free, and the top rate for children is 30%, 8th highest of 28 countries. Rates 7% to 30% for close family; generous reliefs for family homes and businesses.
