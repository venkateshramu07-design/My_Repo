Minimum Wage in Norway (2026)

Norway has no statutory national minimum wage. Pay floors are set sector by sector in collective agreements between unions and employers, which cover most workers and are often higher than the legal minimums of neighbouring countries.

Income Tax and Take-Home Pay in Norway

A single worker on the average wage in Norway (about $88,000 at purchasing-power parity) pays 28.1% of gross pay in income tax and employee social security, so they keep 72%. That is 12th highest of 38 countries, against an OECD average of 25.1%.

Income tax and employee social security take 28.1% of pay at the average wage, 23.5% at two-thirds of it and 35.1% at 1.67 times it. Including employer contributions, the total tax wedge is 36.4% of labour costs.

Electricity Prices in Norway

Households in Norway pay about €0.199 per kWh of electricity (about $0.22), 9th cheapest of 33 countries. Change: +3.7% vs late 2025.

Typical household using 2,500–5,000 kWh a year, all taxes included, January–June 2026.

Retirement Age in Norway

The state pension age in Norway is 67 for people reaching it in 2026; the earliest you can claim is 62 (flexible). Future change: Linked to life expectancy from 2029.

How Much the State Pension Pays in Norway

A full-career average earner in Norway can expect a mandatory pension worth about 54.9% of their previous net pay, 20th least generous of 50 countries (OECD average 63.2%). Low earner (half the average wage): 74%. High earner (twice the average): 37%.

How Much of Your Money Is Protected in Norway

Bank deposits in Norway are protected up to NOK 2 million per depositor, per bank (about $213,900), under the Bankenes sikringsfond scheme. For banks in the Norwegian scheme. Money in payment apps and e-money wallets is usually not covered.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Norway have a minimum wage?

No. Norway has no national minimum wage set by law; minimum pay is agreed sector by sector in collective agreements.

How much tax do you pay in Norway?

On the average wage, income tax plus employee social security in Norway take 28.1% of gross pay, so a single worker keeps 72% (OECD average: 25.1%).

How much does electricity cost in Norway?

Household electricity in Norway costs about €0.199 per kWh (about $0.22), 9th cheapest of 33 countries.

What is the retirement age in Norway?

The state pension age in Norway is 67 in 2026, with the earliest claim at 62 (flexible). Legislated change: Linked to life expectancy from 2029.

How much is the state pension in Norway?

In Norway, mandatory pensions replace about 54.9% of an average earner's net pay after a full career, against an OECD average of 63.2%.

How much money is insured in a bank in Norway?

Deposits in Norway are insured up to NOK 2 million per person, per bank.

Is there inheritance tax in Norway?

No. Norway does not tax inheritances passed to children. Abolished in 2014.
