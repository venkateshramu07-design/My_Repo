Minimum Wage in India (2026)

The legal minimum wage in India is ₹178/day (national floor). For a month of full-time work that is about $49 at current exchange rates, the lowest of 38 countries we compare in our comparison. Non-binding national floor; states set their own, often higher, rates.

Pay Rises in India: 2027 Forecast

Employers in India expect to raise salaries by 8.9% in 2027 (median of the major pay surveys), after 9.5% in 2026. With inflation at 5.5% (Sep), that is a real rise of about 3.2 points.

Mortgage Rates in India (2026)

The typical mortgage rate in India is 7.25% (bank home loans, 2026), 4th highest of 31 countries.

SBI's starting rate in mid-2026, before the RBI raised the repo rate to 5.50% on 7 October 2026; floating rates reset with the repo rate.

Retirement Age in India

The state pension age in India is 58 (EPS pension) for people reaching it in 2026; the earliest you can claim is 50 (reduced). Future change: none legislated. No universal state pension. The EPFO pension starts at 58; most government jobs retire at 60.

How Much the State Pension Pays in India

A full-career average earner in India can expect a mandatory pension worth about 44.6% of their previous net pay, 9th least generous of 50 countries (OECD average 63.2%). Low earner (half the average wage): 45%. High earner (twice the average): 24%.

How Much of Your Money Is Protected in India

Bank deposits in India are protected up to ₹5 lakh (₹500,000) per depositor, per bank (about $5,300), under the DICGC scheme. Money in payment apps and e-money wallets is usually not covered.

Inheritance Tax in India

India has no inheritance tax for children. Estate duty abolished in 1985.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum wage in India in 2026?

The minimum wage in India is ₹178/day (national floor), about $49 a month for full-time work. That is the lowest of 38 countries we compare. Non-binding national floor; states set their own, often higher, rates.

What is the average pay rise in India for 2027?

Pay surveys forecast an average salary increase of 8.9% in India for 2027, against inflation of 5.5%.

What are mortgage rates in India right now?

Mortgage rates in India are about 7.25% (bank home loans, 2026). SBI's starting rate in mid-2026, before the RBI raised the repo rate to 5.50% on 7 October 2026; floating rates reset with the repo rate.

What is the retirement age in India?

The state pension age in India is 58 (EPS pension) in 2026, with the earliest claim at 50 (reduced). Legislated change: None legislated.

How much is the state pension in India?

In India, mandatory pensions replace about 44.6% of an average earner's net pay after a full career, against an OECD average of 63.2%.

How much money is insured in a bank in India?

Deposits in India are insured up to ₹5 lakh (₹500,000) per person, per bank.

Is there inheritance tax in India?

No. India does not tax inheritances passed to children. Estate duty abolished in 1985.
