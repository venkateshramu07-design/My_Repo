Minimum Wage in Greece (2026)

The legal minimum wage in Greece is €1,073/month. For a month of full-time work that is about $1,201 at current exchange rates, 19th highest of 38 countries in our comparison.

Income Tax and Take-Home Pay in Greece

A single worker on the average wage in Greece (about $51,000 at purchasing-power parity) pays 26.1% of gross pay in income tax and employee social security, so they keep 74%. That is 16th highest of 38 countries, against an OECD average of 25.1%.

Income tax and employee social security take 26.1% of pay at the average wage, 21.1% at two-thirds of it and 33.0% at 1.67 times it. Including employer contributions, the total tax wedge is 39.3% of labour costs.

Electricity Prices in Greece

Households in Greece pay about €0.240 per kWh of electricity (about $0.27), 17th cheapest of 33 countries; the EU average is €0.283. Change: +0.8% vs late 2025.

Household gas: €0.077 per kWh. Typical household using 2,500–5,000 kWh a year, all taxes included, January–June 2026.

Petrol Prices in Greece Since the Iran War

Petrol in Greece costs €2.19/L (week of 19 October 2026), up 26.1% from €1.74/L in the week before the Iran war began. It peaked at €2.21/L (28 Sep). At about $2.45 a litre it is 5th most expensive of 29 countries, and the rise is 14th largest of 29 countries.

How Much the State Pension Pays in Greece

A full-career average earner in Greece can expect a mandatory pension worth about 88.5% of their previous net pay, 6th most generous of 50 countries (OECD average 63.2%). Low earner (half the average wage): 99%. High earner (twice the average): 80%.

How Much of Your Money Is Protected in Greece

Bank deposits in Greece are protected up to €100,000 per depositor, per bank (about $111,900), the limit set by EU law for every member state. Harmonised by EU law; local-currency equivalent outside the euro. Money in payment apps and e-money wallets is usually not covered.

Retiring to Greece From Abroad

Foreign retirees usually move to Greece on the Financially independent person visa, which requires About €3,500 a month (about $3,920 a month). Prices are about 60% of US levels, so $2,000 buys roughly what $3,310 would in the US. Optional 7% flat tax on foreign income, including pensions, for up to 15 years for new residents.
