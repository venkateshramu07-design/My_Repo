Minimum Wage in Ireland (2026)

The legal minimum wage in Ireland is €2,391/month. For a month of full-time work that is about $2,676 at current exchange rates, 4th highest of 38 countries in our comparison. €14.15 an hour from January 2026.

Income Tax and Take-Home Pay in Ireland

A single worker on the average wage in Ireland (about $81,000 at purchasing-power parity) pays 25.1% of gross pay in income tax and employee social security, so they keep 75%. That is 19th highest of 38 countries, against an OECD average of 25.1%.

Income tax and employee social security take 25.1% of pay at the average wage, 16.1% at two-thirds of it and 35.4% at 1.67 times it. Including employer contributions, the total tax wedge is 32.6% of labour costs.

Electricity Prices in Ireland

Households in Ireland pay about €0.387 per kWh of electricity (about $0.43), the most expensive of 33 countries we compare; the EU average is €0.283. Change: −4.2% vs late 2025.

Household gas: €0.118 per kWh. Typical household using 2,500–5,000 kWh a year, all taxes included, January–June 2026.

Petrol Prices in Ireland Since the Iran War

Petrol in Ireland costs €1.97/L (week of 19 October 2026), up 13.3% from €1.74/L in the week before the Iran war began. It peaked at €1.98/L (5 Oct). At about $2.21 a litre it is 11th most expensive of 29 countries, and the rise is 3rd smallest of 29 countries.

How Much the State Pension Pays in Ireland

A full-career average earner in Ireland can expect a mandatory pension worth about 33.7% of their previous net pay, 3rd least generous of 50 countries (OECD average 63.2%). Low earner (half the average wage): 56%. High earner (twice the average): 20%. Including common voluntary private pensions, an average earner would get 72%.

How Much of Your Money Is Protected in Ireland

Bank deposits in Ireland are protected up to €100,000 per depositor, per bank (about $111,900), the limit set by EU law for every member state. Harmonised by EU law; local-currency equivalent outside the euro. Money in payment apps and e-money wallets is usually not covered.

Inheritance Tax in Ireland

Ireland has capital acquisitions tax. Children can receive €420,000 per child (from 7 October 2026) tax-free, and the top rate for children is 33%, 7th highest of 28 countries. Flat 33% above the lifetime threshold; spouses are exempt.
