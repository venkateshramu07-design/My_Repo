Minimum Wage in Australia (2026)

The legal minimum wage in Australia is A$26.44/hour. For a month of full-time work that is about $3,058 at current exchange rates, 2nd highest of 38 countries in our comparison. A$1,004.90 a week (38 hours) from 1 July 2026, up about 6%.

Income Tax and Take-Home Pay in Australia

A single worker on the average wage in Australia (about $79,000 at purchasing-power parity) pays 23.5% of gross pay in income tax and employee social security, so they keep 76%. That is 15th lowest of 38 countries, against an OECD average of 25.1%.

Income tax and employee social security take 23.5% of pay at the average wage, 19.3% at two-thirds of it and 28.7% at 1.67 times it. Including employer contributions, the total tax wedge is 27.9% of labour costs.

Pay Rises in Australia: 2027 Forecast

Employers in Australia expect to raise salaries by 3.3% in 2027 (median of the major pay surveys), after 3.4% in 2026. With inflation at 4.0% (Aug), that is a real cut of about 0.7 points unless inflation falls.

Mortgage Rates in Australia (2026)

The typical mortgage rate in Australia is 6.49% (average variable, Oct 2026), 5th highest of 31 countries.

Canstar's estimate after the RBA raised the cash rate to 4.60% on 29 September 2026. Per A$100,000 over 25 years: A$675 a month.

Retirement Age in Australia

The state pension age in Australia is 67 (Age Pension) for people reaching it in 2026; the earliest you can claim is 60 (superannuation). Future change: none legislated. Super can be accessed from the preservation age of 60 after retiring.

How Much the State Pension Pays in Australia

A full-career average earner in Australia can expect a mandatory pension worth about 53% of their previous net pay, 15th least generous of 50 countries (OECD average 63.2%). Low earner (half the average wage): 83%. High earner (twice the average): 38%.

How Much of Your Money Is Protected in Australia

Bank deposits in Australia are protected up to A$250,000 per depositor, per bank (about $175,600), under the Financial Claims Scheme scheme. Money in payment apps and e-money wallets is usually not covered.

Inheritance Tax in Australia

Australia has no inheritance tax for children. Abolished in the late 1970s; heirs take on the capital gains tax position of the assets.
