Minimum Wage in Brazil (2026)

The legal minimum wage in Brazil is R$1,621/month. For a month of full-time work that is about $321 at current exchange rates, 5th lowest of 38 countries in our comparison. From 1 January 2026, up 6.8%.

The typical mortgage rate in Brazil is 11.19% (Caixa lowest rate, 2026), the highest of 31 countries we compare.

How Much the State Pension Pays in Brazil

A full-career average earner in Brazil can expect a mandatory pension worth about 97.5% of their previous net pay, 2nd most generous of 50 countries (OECD average 63.2%). Low earner (half the average wage): 96%. High earner (twice the average): 88%.

How Much of Your Money Is Protected in Brazil

Bank deposits in Brazil are protected up to R$250,000 per depositor, per bank (about $49,500), under the FGC scheme. Money in payment apps and e-money wallets is usually not covered.

Inheritance Tax in Brazil

Brazil has itcmd (state tax). Children can receive Varies by state tax-free, and the top rate for children is 8%, 13th highest of 28 countries. States set rates up to 8%; the 2023 tax reform requires progressive rates.
