Minimum Wage in China (2026)

The legal minimum wage in China is ¥2,740/month. For a month of full-time work that is about $411 at current exchange rates, 6th lowest of 38 countries in our comparison. Highest provincial rate; set by province.

How Much the State Pension Pays in China

A full-career average earner in China can expect a mandatory pension worth about 103.6% of their previous net pay, the most generous of 50 countries we compare (OECD average 63.2%). Low earner (half the average wage): 130%. High earner (twice the average): 91%.

How Much of Your Money Is Protected in China

Bank deposits in China are protected up to ¥500,000 (RMB) per depositor, per bank (about $75,000), under the Deposit insurance fund scheme. Principal and interest. Money in payment apps and e-money wallets is usually not covered.
