How to Use These Comparisons

  • Wages and tax together: a high minimum wage can come with high tax. Compare the US-dollar minimum wage with the share of pay the average worker keeps.
  • Homes: the mortgage rate sets the monthly payment. A 2-point difference adds about 20% to the payment on a 25-year loan.
  • Bills: electricity prices vary more than threefold. Petrol rises since February show where the Iran war hit hardest.
  • Retirement: look at the pension age and the share of pay the pension replaces together; a later age with a generous pension can beat an early age with a small one.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which country has the highest minimum wage in 2026?

Converted to US dollars for a month of full-time work, the highest minimum wages are in Luxembourg, Australia and the United Kingdom.

Which country takes the most tax from an average worker?

Counting income tax and employee social security on the average wage, the highest are Belgium, Germany and Lithuania; the lowest are Mexico, South Korea and Switzerland.

Where are mortgage rates lowest?

Among these countries, the lowest typical mortgage rates are in Switzerland, Japan and Portugal.

Which country has the cheapest electricity?

The cheapest household electricity, in US dollars per kWh, is in the United States, Slovakia and Norway.

Where is the retirement age lowest?

The lowest state pension ages among these countries are in India, South Africa and China.
