Minimum Wage in South Africa (2026)

The legal minimum wage in South Africa is R30.23/hour. For a month of full-time work that is about $316 at current exchange rates, 4th lowest of 38 countries in our comparison. From 1 March 2026.

How Much the State Pension Pays in South Africa

A full-career average earner in South Africa can expect a mandatory pension worth about 8.9% of their previous net pay, the least generous of 50 countries we compare (OECD average 63.2%). Low earner (half the average wage): 17%. High earner (twice the average): 5%. Including common voluntary private pensions, an average earner would get 32%.

How Much of Your Money Is Protected in South Africa

Bank deposits in South Africa are protected up to R100,000 per depositor, per bank (about $6,000), under the CODI scheme. Scheme launched April 2024. Money in payment apps and e-money wallets is usually not covered.

Inheritance Tax in South Africa

South Africa has estate duty. Children can receive R3.5 million tax-free, and the top rate for children is 25%, 10th highest of 28 countries. 20% above the abatement and 25% on estates above R30 million; spouses exempt.
