Minimum Wage in Canada (2026)

The legal minimum wage in Canada is C$18.15/hour. For a month of full-time work that is about $2,248 at current exchange rates, 9th highest of 38 countries in our comparison. Federally regulated sectors from 1 April 2026; provinces set their own rates.

Income Tax and Take-Home Pay in Canada

A single worker on the average wage in Canada (about $82,000 at purchasing-power parity) pays 25.6% of gross pay in income tax and employee social security, so they keep 74%. That is 18th highest of 38 countries, against an OECD average of 25.1%.

Income tax and employee social security take 25.6% of pay at the average wage, 22.1% at two-thirds of it and 31.1% at 1.67 times it. Including employer contributions, the total tax wedge is 32.1% of labour costs.

Employers in Canada expect to raise salaries by 3.2% in 2027 (median of the major pay surveys), after 3.4% in 2026. With inflation at 3.1% (Sep), that is a real rise of about 0.1 points.

Mortgage Rates in Canada (2026)

The typical mortgage rate in Canada is 5.14% (5-year fixed, big bank, Oct 2026), 7th highest of 31 countries.

BMO's standard 5-year fixed from 7 October; the best broker rates are around 4.1% to 4.4%. Bank of Canada rate 2.25%.

Retirement Age in Canada

The state pension age in Canada is 65 for people reaching it in 2026; the earliest you can claim is 60 (CPP, reduced). Future change: none legislated. CPP can start from 60 to 70; Old Age Security from 65, deferrable to 70.

How Much the State Pension Pays in Canada

A full-career average earner in Canada can expect a mandatory pension worth about 45.1% of their previous net pay, 10th least generous of 50 countries (OECD average 63.2%). Low earner (half the average wage): 56%. High earner (twice the average): 25%. Including common voluntary private pensions, an average earner would get 71%.

How Much of Your Money Is Protected in Canada

Bank deposits in Canada are protected up to C$100,000 per depositor, per bank (about $71,500), under the CDIC scheme. Per eligible category. Money in payment apps and e-money wallets is usually not covered.

Inheritance Tax in Canada

Canada has no inheritance tax for children. Assets are treated as sold at death, so capital gains tax is due on the gain; transfers to a spouse are deferred.

Frequently Asked Questions: Canada Minimum Wage 2026

What is the minimum wage in Canada in 2026?

The minimum wage in Canada is C$18.15/hour, about $2,248 a month for full-time work. That is 9th highest of 38 countries. Federally regulated sectors from 1 April 2026; provinces set their own rates.

How much tax do you pay in Canada?

On the average wage, income tax plus employee social security in Canada take 25.6% of gross pay, so a single worker keeps 74% (OECD average: 25.1%).

What is the average pay rise in Canada for 2027?

Pay surveys forecast an average salary increase of 3.2% in Canada for 2027, against inflation of 3.1%.
