Minimum Wage in France (2026)

The legal minimum wage in France is €1,867/month. For a month of full-time work that is about $2,090 at current exchange rates, 10th highest of 38 countries in our comparison. SMIC; raised in 2026.

Income Tax and Take-Home Pay in France

A single worker on the average wage in France (about $67,000 at purchasing-power parity) pays 28% of gross pay in income tax and employee social security, so they keep 72%. That is 13th highest of 38 countries, against an OECD average of 25.1%.

Income tax and employee social security take 28.0% of pay at the average wage, 23.7% at two-thirds of it and 33.8% at 1.67 times it. Including employer contributions, the total tax wedge is 47.2% of labour costs.

Pay Rises in France: 2027 Forecast

Employers in France expect to raise salaries by 2.9% in 2027 (median of the major pay surveys), after 3.1% in 2026. With inflation at 3.4% (Sep), that is a real cut of about 0.5 points unless inflation falls.

Mortgage Rates in France (2026)

The typical mortgage rate in France is 3.2% (new home loans, August 2026), 8th lowest of 31 countries; the euro area average is 3.6%.

Up 0.10 points since February 2026 (3.10%). Monthly payment per €100,000 over 25 years: €485.

Electricity Prices in France

Households in France pay about €0.248 per kWh of electricity (about $0.28), 14th most expensive of 33 countries; the EU average is €0.283. Change: −3.0% vs late 2025.

Household gas: €0.135 per kWh. Typical household using 2,500–5,000 kWh a year, all taxes included, January–June 2026.

Petrol Prices in France Since the Iran War

Petrol in France costs €2.23/L (week of 19 October 2026), up 30.4% from €1.71/L in the week before the Iran war began. It peaked at €2.23/L (19 Oct). At about $2.49 a litre it is 4th most expensive of 29 countries, and the rise is 11th largest of 29 countries.

Retirement Age in France

The state pension age in France is 62 y 9 m for people reaching it in 2026; the earliest you can claim is Earlier for long careers. Future change: Reform frozen until January 2028; 64 for those born 1969+. The 2026 social security law froze the 2023 reform at 62 y 9 m. A full-rate pension is automatic at 67.

How Much the State Pension Pays in France

A full-career average earner in France can expect a mandatory pension worth about 70% of their previous net pay, 23rd most generous of 50 countries (OECD average 63.2%). Low earner (half the average wage): 66%. High earner (twice the average): 59%.

How Much of Your Money Is Protected in France

Bank deposits in France are protected up to €100,000 per depositor, per bank (about $111,900), the limit set by EU law for every member state. Harmonised by EU law; local-currency equivalent outside the euro. Money in payment apps and e-money wallets is usually not covered.

Inheritance Tax in France

France has inheritance tax. Children can receive €100,000 per child tax-free, and the top rate for children is 45%, 3rd highest of 28 countries. Rates 5% to 45% above about €1.8 million per child; spouses are exempt. Rates for other relatives reach 60%.

Retiring to France From Abroad

Foreign retirees usually move to France on the Long-stay visitor visa, which requires No fixed figure (usually at least the net minimum wage). Prices are about 78% of US levels, so $2,000 buys roughly what $2,580 would in the US. Pensions taxed under tax treaties; US Social Security is taxed only in the US.
