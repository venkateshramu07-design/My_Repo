Minimum Wage in New Zealand (2026)

The legal minimum wage in New Zealand is NZ$23.95/hour. For a month of full-time work that is about $2,361 at current exchange rates, 8th highest of 38 countries in our comparison. Adult rate from 1 April 2026.

Income Tax and Take-Home Pay in New Zealand

A single worker on the average wage in New Zealand (about $57,000 at purchasing-power parity) pays 20.8% of gross pay in income tax and employee social security, so they keep 79%. That is 7th lowest of 38 countries, against an OECD average of 25.1%.

Income tax and employee social security take 20.8% of pay at the average wage, 15.1% at two-thirds of it and 25.7% at 1.67 times it. Including employer contributions, the total tax wedge is 20.8% of labour costs.

How Much the State Pension Pays in New Zealand

A full-career average earner in New Zealand can expect a mandatory pension worth about 43.8% of their previous net pay, 8th least generous of 50 countries (OECD average 63.2%). Low earner (half the average wage): 67%. High earner (twice the average): 24%. Including common voluntary private pensions, an average earner would get 69%.

How Much of Your Money Is Protected in New Zealand

Bank deposits in New Zealand are protected up to NZ$100,000 per depositor, per bank (about $56,900), under the Depositor Compensation Scheme scheme. Scheme started 1 July 2025. Money in payment apps and e-money wallets is usually not covered.
