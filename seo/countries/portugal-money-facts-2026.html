Minimum Wage in Portugal (2026)

The legal minimum wage in Portugal is €1,073/month. For a month of full-time work that is about $1,201 at current exchange rates, 19th lowest of 38 countries in our comparison.

Income Tax and Take-Home Pay in Portugal

A single worker on the average wage in Portugal (about $46,000 at purchasing-power parity) pays 24.9% of gross pay in income tax and employee social security, so they keep 75%. That is 19th lowest of 38 countries, against an OECD average of 25.1%.

Income tax and employee social security take 24.9% of pay at the average wage, 20.9% at two-thirds of it and 32.2% at 1.67 times it. Including employer contributions, the total tax wedge is 39.3% of labour costs.

Electricity Prices in Portugal

Households in Portugal pay about €0.237 per kWh of electricity (about $0.27), 15th cheapest of 33 countries; the EU average is €0.283. Change: −2.8% vs late 2025.

Household gas: €0.126 per kWh. Typical household using 2,500–5,000 kWh a year, all taxes included, January–June 2026.

Petrol Prices in Portugal Since the Iran War

Petrol in Portugal costs €2.10/L (week of 19 October 2026), up 24.5% from €1.69/L in the week before the Iran war began. It peaked at €2.11/L (12 Oct). At about $2.35 a litre it is 7th most expensive of 29 countries, and the rise is 15th smallest of 29 countries.

How Much the State Pension Pays in Portugal

A full-career average earner in Portugal can expect a mandatory pension worth about 92.7% of their previous net pay, 5th most generous of 50 countries (OECD average 63.2%). Low earner (half the average wage): 91%. High earner (twice the average): 94%.

How Much of Your Money Is Protected in Portugal

Bank deposits in Portugal are protected up to €100,000 per depositor, per bank (about $111,900), the limit set by EU law for every member state. Harmonised by EU law; local-currency equivalent outside the euro. Money in payment apps and e-money wallets is usually not covered.

Inheritance Tax in Portugal

Portugal has no inheritance tax for children. 10% stamp duty applies to other heirs.

Retiring to Portugal From Abroad

Foreign retirees usually move to Portugal on the D7 passive income visa, which requires €920 a month (+50% spouse) (about $1,030 a month). Prices are about 60% of US levels, so $2,000 buys roughly what $3,310 would in the US. Foreign pensions taxed at normal Portuguese rates since the NHR regime closed to new applicants in 2024.
