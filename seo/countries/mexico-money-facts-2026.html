Minimum Wage in Mexico (2026)

The legal minimum wage in Mexico is MX$315.04/day. For a month of full-time work that is about $510 at current exchange rates, 7th lowest of 38 countries in our comparison. General rate from 1 January 2026, up 13%; higher in the northern border zone.

Income Tax and Take-Home Pay in Mexico

A single worker on the average wage in Mexico (about $21,000 at purchasing-power parity) pays 13.2% of gross pay in income tax and employee social security, so they keep 87%. That is 4th lowest of 38 countries, against an OECD average of 25.1%.

Income tax and employee social security take 13.2% of pay at the average wage, 9.6% at two-thirds of it and 17.1% at 1.67 times it. Including employer contributions, the total tax wedge is 21.7% of labour costs.

Mortgage Rates in Mexico (2026)

The typical mortgage rate in Mexico is 10.8% (bank fixed rates, 2026), 2nd highest of 31 countries.

Retirement Age in Mexico

The state pension age in Mexico is 65 for people reaching it in 2026; the earliest you can claim is 60 (reduced). Future change: none legislated. IMSS old-age pension; a reduced 'cesantía' pension from 60.

How Much the State Pension Pays in Mexico

A full-career average earner in Mexico can expect a mandatory pension worth about 79.6% of their previous net pay, 11th most generous of 50 countries (OECD average 63.2%). Low earner (half the average wage): 132%. High earner (twice the average): 57%.

How Much of Your Money Is Protected in Mexico

Bank deposits in Mexico are protected up to 400,000 UDIs (≈ MX$3.5m) per depositor, per bank (about $186,300), under the IPAB scheme. Set in inflation-linked UDIs; peso value changes daily. Money in payment apps and e-money wallets is usually not covered.

Retiring to Mexico From Abroad

Foreign retirees usually move to Mexico on the Temporary residency, which requires About $4,400–4,700 a month or about $75,000 in savings (about $4,500 a month). Prices are about 59% of US levels, so $2,000 buys roughly what $3,390 would in the US. Residents can be taxed on worldwide income; many foreign pensions are covered by tax treaties.
