Minimum Wage in the Netherlands (2026)

The legal minimum wage in the Netherlands is €2,338/month. For a month of full-time work that is about $2,617 at current exchange rates, 6th highest of 38 countries in our comparison.

Income Tax and Take-Home Pay in the Netherlands

A single worker on the average wage in the Netherlands (about $93,000 at purchasing-power parity) pays 27.9% of gross pay in income tax and employee social security, so they keep 72%. That is 14th highest of 38 countries, against an OECD average of 25.1%.

Income tax and employee social security take 27.9% of pay at the average wage, 18.3% at two-thirds of it and 37.2% at 1.67 times it. Including employer contributions, the total tax wedge is 35.9% of labour costs.

Electricity Prices in the Netherlands

Households in the Netherlands pay about €0.244 per kWh of electricity (about $0.27), 15th most expensive of 33 countries; the EU average is €0.283. Change: −4.4% vs late 2025.

Household gas: €0.162 per kWh. Typical household using 2,500–5,000 kWh a year, all taxes included, January–June 2026.

Petrol Prices in the Netherlands Since the Iran War

Petrol in the Netherlands costs €2.39/L (week of 19 October 2026), up 16% from €2.06/L in the week before the Iran war began. It peaked at €2.47/L (7 Sep). At about $2.68 a litre it is 2nd most expensive of 29 countries, and the rise is 5th smallest of 29 countries.

How Much the State Pension Pays in the Netherlands

A full-career average earner in the Netherlands can expect a mandatory pension worth about 96% of their previous net pay, 4th most generous of 50 countries (OECD average 63.2%). Low earner (half the average wage): 97%. High earner (twice the average): 90%.

How Much of Your Money Is Protected in the Netherlands

Bank deposits in the Netherlands are protected up to €100,000 per depositor, per bank (about $111,900), the limit set by EU law for every member state. Harmonised by EU law; local-currency equivalent outside the euro. Money in payment apps and e-money wallets is usually not covered.

Inheritance Tax in the Netherlands

The Netherlands has inheritance tax. Children can receive About €26,000 per child tax-free, and the top rate for children is 20%, 11th highest of 28 countries. 10% up to about €150,000 and 20% above; spouses get a larger exemption.
