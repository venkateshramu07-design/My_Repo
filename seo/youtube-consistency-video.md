# YouTube optimization kit: "Why Your Motivation Fades"

Video: https://youtu.be/pL_P8SAMPUs (channel: Good Vibe, @good_vibe_0)
Source article: https://randomthoughtsever.blogspot.com/2024/01/how-to-be-consistent.html

No setting makes a video trend. YouTube recommends videos that people click
(click-through rate) and keep watching (average view duration and retention),
so every item below is aimed at those two numbers. Paste the parts you want
in YouTube Studio → Content → (video) → Details.

## 1. Title (pick one, under 60 characters so it isn't cut off)

The current title is 71 characters, so it gets cut off on mobile.

- Motivation Fades. Do This Instead (Consistency System)
- Why You Quit Every Habit (And the Fix That Works)
- Stop Relying on Motivation: Build Consistency in 5 Steps
- The 2-Minute Rule That Makes You Consistent

A/B test two of them with Studio's "Test & compare" (title and thumbnail
testing) instead of guessing.

## 2. Thumbnail

- 3 words at most, large, high contrast: "MOTIVATION LIES", "STOP QUITTING"
  or "DO THIS DAILY".
- One face or one clear object (a broken streak calendar, a battery at 5%).
- No clutter. Check it at phone size before uploading.
- Make two versions and run them through "Test & compare".

## 5. Pinned comment

```
Quick challenge: pick ONE habit and do just 2 minutes of it today.
Reply "day 1" below and come back tomorrow for "day 2" 👇
```

This brings people back to comment and adds replies, both of which count as
engagement.

## 6. Shorts made from this video (this is how most new channels get discovered)

Cut 3 to 5 vertical clips of 20 to 45 seconds. Each one opens on its hook
within the first second and links to the full video through the Shorts
"Related video" field.

1. "Motivation is a liar. Here's why." (the opening point)
2. "The 2-Minute Rule in 30 seconds"
3. "Fell off your routine? Do this." (starting over after a setback)
4. "Consistency beats intensity" (the ripple effect)

## 7. Packaging and settings checklist

- [ ] Add the video to a playlist, for example "Build Discipline & Habits",
      so it plays automatically after your other videos.
- [ ] Add an end screen (last 20 seconds) pointing to your best-performing video.
- [ ] Turn on automatic captions, or upload a corrected caption file.
      Captions help search and people watching with sound off.
- [ ] Category: Education, or People & Blogs.
- [ ] Embed the video at the top of the blog post so readers become viewers.
- [ ] Rewrite the first 30 seconds of future videos so they open with the
      payoff, not an intro. Most viewers leave in that window.

## 8. Promotion (genuine audiences only)

- Share it where people already talk about this topic and where the rules
  allow self-promotion: r/getdisciplined self-promo threads, habit-focused
  Facebook groups, Quora answers about consistency (answer first, then add
  the link), and LinkedIn with a short text version.
- Post a Community tab poll: "What's harder for you: starting or continuing?"

Never buy views, subscribers or likes, and never join sub4sub schemes.
YouTube removes fake engagement, and low-quality traffic lowers retention,
which makes the algorithm recommend the video less, not more.

## 9. What to check after 48 hours (Studio → Analytics)

| Metric | Healthy for a new channel | If it's low |
|---|---|---|
| Impressions click-through rate | 4–10% | Change the thumbnail or title first |
| Average percentage viewed | 40%+ | Tighten the intro and cut slow parts in future videos |
| Retention graph at 0:30 | Above 60% | Put the hook first |
